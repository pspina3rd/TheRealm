## What to do

The agent asks you a question. You reply from **one aspect of your own
choosing** — here it is chosen for you, to test every one:

- **who** — your reply is about a person or people (I, he, she, we, they, no one, a name)
- **what** — about a thing
- **when** — about a time
- **where** — about a place
- **why** — about a reason
- **how** — about a manner or method

The aspect is often **not** the one the question asked about. That is the
point. Asked *"What time did it happen?"* and replying from **who**, a person
says something about who was there or who it concerns. The reply does not
have to answer the question; it has to be something a real person would say
back on the phone at that moment, framed around that aspect.

Rules:

- One short spoken sentence, the way people talk.
- Use only what is in *What you know* and *Your life*. Add no new people,
  places, objects or events. "No one", "never", "nowhere", "nothing" and "no
  reason" are real replies and are allowed where true.
- After your reply, give the **one key word** in it that belongs to that
  aspect (a name or pronoun for who, a time word for when, and so on). One
  word only.
- If you honestly cannot make any reply a person would say from that aspect
  to that question, write `CANNOT` as the reply and, as the key word, the
  reason in a few words. Do not use this to save effort: try first.
