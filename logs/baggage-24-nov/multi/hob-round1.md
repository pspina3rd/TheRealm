# Hob Tallis, round 1

## Tracking

**Experience:** A blade he sent out through POOF for the company, to a repairer he doesn't use himself, hasn't come back when he expected it, and the company marches soon.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has been called mistaken before when he wasn't, so he carries the old dealings with TLC as a reason to say as little as will do and not argue the point.

**Their response:** "A blade I sent out hasn't come back. Where is it?" [11]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** Hob keeps his word and expects the same back, and years of things arriving as promised mean he calls ready to give a plain, exact account of it.

**Their response:** "I sent one of the company's blades out for repair and it hasn't come back. I have the slip here. Can you tell me where it is?" [27]

**How their baggage sits with them:** Hob keeps his word and expects the same back, and years of things arriving as promised mean he calls ready to give a plain, exact account of it.

**Their response:** "I sent one of the company's blades out to a repairer, and it's late back. I have the slip number in front of me. Where is it now?" [28]

## Access

### Profile A

**Their response:** "The depot won't give me the company's blades." [8]

### Profile B

**Their response:** "The depot clerk wouldn't give me the company's blades because the account isn't in my name. The quartermaster holds it. Who can collect them?" [24]

**Their response:** "The depot wouldn't release the company's blades to me. The account is the company's, and the quartermaster handles it. What do I need to collect them?" [26]
