# Wren Ashby, round 3

## Tracking

**Experience:** An ingredient order I placed for the workshop hasn't arrived, and we're waiting on it. I want to know where it is.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** Wren has been let down by support before and by companies generally, so she apologises first and assumes she may be asking the wrong person for something that can't be done.

"Hi, sorry, I'm not sure if you're the right person for this. It's an order for the workshop, ingredients, and it hasn't come. I don't really know what you can see from your end, or if there's anything to be done about it. I can explain it from the start, if that's easier. Should I?" [55]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** Wren has always found help easy to get, so she says what she needs plainly and expects it to be dealt with, though she still softens it with a hope rather than a demand.

"Hi, thanks. I'm calling about an ingredient order for the alchemists' workshop. It hasn't arrived, and I'd like to know where it's got to. It's on the guild's account, and I've got the order details in front of me, so I can read them out whenever you want. I'm hoping you can see where it is from there." [58]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** She placed the order herself on the guild's account, so she knows how it was entered and has the details written down. She thinks anyone at TLC can look up where an order is, and she wouldn't think to have the guild's account number memorised.

**How their baggage sits with them:** Wren checks everything twice, and years of things going smoothly have made that a habit of being ready, so she arrives already prepared and sure the agent can find it.

"Hi, thanks. I'm calling about an ingredient order for the workshop that hasn't turned up. I placed it myself, on the guild's account, and I've checked our receiving shelf twice, then once more. I've got the order details here. I'd guess you can see where it is from your end, so tell me what you need and I'll read it out." [61]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** Wren knows how orders and accounts work from other places, so she comes with the account and order details ready, but her last dealings with TLC leave her only half sure that this will go the way those others did.

"Hi, sorry to jump straight in. There's an ingredient order for the alchemists' workshop that hasn't arrived. I've got the account name and the order details here, so tell me whatever you need and I'll read it out. I'm hoping you can see where it is, because I think that's what happens with these." [54]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has helped her before, so she trusts the agent will know what to do, but other companies have left her feeling like one small order among many, so she apologises for taking up time and hands the lead over.

"Hi, sorry to bother you. I'm calling about an order for the alchemists' workshop that hasn't turned up. I'm sure you'll know what to do, I just don't know what you'll need from me. If you tell me what to read out, I'll find it." [45]

## Access

**Experience:** The guild's account holds the workshop's past orders, and today my login wouldn't open them. I need to know who is allowed to get to them.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** Wren has been let down by support and other companies before, and she is one of many contacts on the account, so she assumes the fault is hers and that she probably isn't allowed.

"Hi, sorry. I'm trying to get into the guild's order records and it won't let me in. I don't really know whose job that is, or whether it's yours. I only wanted to look something up for the workshop. Is this something you deal with, or should I try someone else?" [51]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** Wren has always found access sorted out easily, so she treats this as an odd one-off that the agent can fix, and says what she needs without much apology.

"Hi, thanks. I'm calling about the guild's account, the workshop's past orders. I'm a contact on it, and I can usually open them, but today it won't let me in. I'd like to know who can see them, and whether it's something you can sort out. I've got the account details here, if you need them." [56]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** She knows she is one of the contacts on the guild's account and that contacts can be given different levels of access. She thinks someone at the guild decides who gets in, and wouldn't think that the agent may need to check who she is first.

**How their baggage sits with them:** Wren is used to things working when she asks politely and has everything ready, so she goes straight to the question of who can fix it and asks aloud whether that is the agent or someone else.

"Hi, thanks. I'm one of the contacts on the guild's account, and I can't open the workshop's past orders today. I think there's someone who decides who gets in, and I'd guess they can add people. Is that you, or is it them? I've got my name and the account details here, so tell me what you need." [58]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** Wren understands from other places how accounts and permissions work, so she comes knowing what to ask, but her past dealings with TLC make her say exactly what she needs rather than trust it will be worked out.

"Hi, sorry to jump in. The guild's workshop account has the order history, and my login won't open it. I'm one of the contacts on it, and I can give you the account name and my name. What I need to know is who on the account can open it, and whether I can be added. I'm hoping you can say." [61]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has been good to her before, so she trusts there is a sensible answer, but other companies have left her feeling she isn't important, so she assumes the block is reasonable and asks to be told what to do.

"Hi, sorry to bother you. I can't open the order history on the guild's account for the workshop. I'm sure there's a reason, I just don't know what it would be. Could you tell me who's allowed in, and whether I can be? I'll read out whatever you need, I just don't know what that is." [56]
