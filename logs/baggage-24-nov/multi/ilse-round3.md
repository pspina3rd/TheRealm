# Ilse Harrow, round 3

## Tracking

**Experience:** She ordered a crate of stock for the shop and it has not arrived when it should have. The last she could see was that it had left a depot.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** Her last dealings with support left her expecting to do the work herself, so she keeps it short, sticks to what she can say for certain, and does not lean on anyone to chase it.

**Their response:** "I'm waiting on a delivery of stock for the shop, and it's late. I don't have a reference, only that it was ordered a while back. Can you tell me where it is right now? Not when it'll come, just where it is." [43]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** Support has always come through for her and she has found other companies the same, so she treats this as a small hitch and gives what she has with an easy confidence.

**Their response:** "Hello. A delivery of stock for my shop hasn't arrived yet. I have the order reference here, and the tracking shows it left a depot, then nothing since. Could you see where it's got to?" [35]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** She reads her own tracking page and keeps her paperwork in order, so she'd have the order reference, the date it was promised, and the last place it was scanned. She'd take a gap in scans for a missed scan, and not think to ask whether it's waiting for a signature or on another carrier's leg.

**How their baggage sits with them:** Her ease comes from twenty years of orders arriving and calls being answered, so she goes straight to a diagnosis and expects it to be a quick look.

**Their response:** "Morning. A delivery for the shop hasn't turned up. The tracking says it left the depot and there's been no scan since, which I'd put down to a missed scan. I have the reference here. Could you look at where it last was?" [43]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** Being let down by support before, while other companies have served her well, makes her lay everything out in one go so nothing has to be asked twice.

**Their response:** "A delivery of stock for my shop is overdue. I have the order reference and the date it was promised, and the tracking stopped at a depot scan. I'd like to know where it physically is, and who to speak to if it isn't moving." [45]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** Support has looked after her before, but other companies have taught her to expect little, so she is polite and hopeful and unsure what she is expected to bring.

**Their response:** "Hello. I'm hoping you can help me find a delivery. It's stock for the shop and it hasn't come. I only have the order details from the supplier, and I'm not sure what you'll need from me. If you can find it, I'd be grateful." [45]

## Access

**Experience:** Her husband Aldo keeps the shop's books and needs to see the shop's account, but she is the only contact on it, so he can't.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** A callback that never came taught her not to count on anyone following up, so she asks plainly and keeps her hopes small.

**Their response:** "My husband does the books for the shop and needs to see our account. I'm the only one on it. I don't know if that can be changed, or how. I'd like him to be able to look. Is that something you can do?" [44]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** A long run of good service makes her assume this is routine, so she says exactly what she wants and that she can prove who she is.

**Their response:** "Hello. My husband keeps the books for the shop and needs to see our account, the invoices and statements. I'm the only contact on it, so I'd like to add him. I have my account details here, ready." [38]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** She is the shop's only contact, so she'd know it all rests on her and that she can authorise a change, and she'd expect to confirm her own details. She wouldn't think to ask whether Aldo needs his own verification or login separate from hers.

**How their baggage sits with them:** Years of things working when she asks has her treating this as paperwork she can finish in one call.

**Their response:** "I'd like to add my husband, Aldo, to the shop's account so he can see the invoices for the books. I'm the only contact on it. I have my details ready, and I expect he'll need to be checked as well." [41]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** Support let her down once, but other companies have always made this kind of thing easy, so she expects it should be simple and wants to know exactly what it takes.

**Their response:** "My husband keeps the books and needs to see the shop's account. I'm the only person on it, so I take it I have to authorise him. I have the account number. What exactly does he need to do? I'd rather not share my own login." [46]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** Support has helped her before, but other companies left her feeling like a small account, so she is hopeful with them and careful not to ask for too much.

**Their response:** "Hello, I'm hoping you can help. My husband does the shop's books and needs to look at our account, but it's only in my name. I'm not sure what you'd need from either of us. Could we sort that out today?" [41]
