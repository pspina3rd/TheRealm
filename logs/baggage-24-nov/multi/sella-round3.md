# Sella Brandt, round 3

## Tracking

**Experience:** A box of salves and bandages for the Ryle brothers, who are still laid up, went out through the guild hall depot and should have reached her party by now. Nobody has told her where it is.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** Years of being passed between counters and clerks have taught Sella to say the least that will do and to see whether anyone is actually going to pick it up.

"A delivery for my party hasn't come. It's overdue. Can you find out where it is, or is that someone else?" [21]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** Sella has had things sorted quickly before, so she hands over what she has in order and expects it to be dealt with, the way she'd brief her own party.

"Hi. I'm waiting on a box for my party, medical supplies for two of my people who are laid up. It went out through the guild hall depot and it's past when it should be here. Can you tell me where it's sitting?" [43]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** She knows the box left from the guild hall depot, that the clerk stamped the slip she kept, and that things for her party normally turn up within a few days. She thinks it stalled at the hand-off at the guild hall, and she wouldn't think to have the route or whatever the depot calls its statuses.

**How their baggage sits with them:** Having been well looked after before, Sella treats this as one stuck step in a system she trusts, and she is sure of what she has seen but not of why it stopped.

"Hi. I've got a box of salves and bandages for my party, sent from the guild hall depot, and it's overdue. I have the slip the clerk stamped. I think it stalled at the hand-off, but I couldn't tell you why. Can you see where it is?" [47]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** Sella has had good dealings elsewhere and has the facts straight, so she comes prepared and points at the one thing she needs, without leaning on anyone to be helpful.

"Hi. A box for my party hasn't turned up. It went out from the guild hall depot, and I have the slip in front of me. I know where it started, I just need to know where it is now." [40]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** Sella has found TLC reliable when she's called before, but plenty of other places have treated her as a nuisance, so she keeps it short and leaves it to the agent to say what's needed.

"Hi. A delivery for my party hasn't shown up. It's medical supplies for the Ryle brothers, they're laid up. Just tell me what you need from me and I'll find it." [31]

## Access

**Experience:** Pell joined her party in the spring and still can't open the party's strongbox at the guild hall. Only Sella can, and she wants that changed.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** Sella has been sent from clerk to clerk over forms before, so she says the problem flatly and waits to see whether this person does anything about it.

"My party member can't get into our strongbox at the guild hall. I need that fixed. Is that something you do?" [21]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** Having had access sorted promptly before, Sella says plainly what she wants done and expects to be told what comes next.

"Hi. One of my party, Pell, needs access to our strongbox at the guild hall. Right now it's only me on it. I'd like Pell added so the box opens for him, same as for me. What do you need from me to do that?" [45]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** She knows the box sits on her dues account through the Adventurers' Guild, that adding someone means a form through the clerk, and that she handed it in during the spring. She believes it's stuck somewhere, and she wouldn't think to know who actually changes access.

**How their baggage sits with them:** Sella has seen how this paperwork is meant to go and trusts it, so the only thing she can't explain is why her form never came back.

"Hi. I need Pell added to our party's strongbox at the guild hall. It's on my dues account, and I gave the clerk the form in the spring. It never came back stamped, so I think it's stuck somewhere. Can you check?" [42]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** Sella knows how the dues paperwork works and has been served well by other outfits, so she lays out the one problem and leaves the agent to prove themselves.

"Hi. Pell joined my party in the spring and still can't open our strongbox at the guild hall. I've got the dues paperwork and I know there's a form with the clerk. I want Pell on the box." [38]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has come through for her before, but being brushed off by other places has made her keep it brief and let the agent say what they need.

"Hi. Someone in my party needs to get into our strongbox at the guild hall and can't. It's under my name. I'd like to fix that, so what do you need from me?" [33]
