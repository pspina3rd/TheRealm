# Hob Tallis, round 2

## Tracking

**Experience:** I sent one of the company's blades out through POOF to a repairer, for work I can't do at my own forge, and it hasn't come back when I expected it. The company marches soon and needs it.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has been told he was mistaken when he wasn't, and with nothing good behind him from TLC or anyone else, he gives the plain fact and nothing a person could pick at.

**Their response:** "I sent a company blade out for repair. It hasn't come back." [12]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** Hob is used to things arriving when they should, so a late blade is the one thing out of place, and he sets it out in order the way he would for a man he expects to act on it.

**Their response:** "I've got a company blade that went out through POOF for repair and hasn't come back. I have the order reference here. Can you tell me where it is?" [29]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** He knows the blade went out on a POOF order for work he can't do at his forge, that the slip has an order reference on it, and that the company's account is paid up. He doesn't think about who the repairer is on TLC's side or how the pickup was recorded.

**How their baggage sits with them:** A man who has had good service and pays on time reads a late blade as a slip, not a pattern, and gives the facts he has in the order they matter.

**Their response:** "A company blade went out through POOF for a repair I couldn't do here. It should be back by now. The order reference is on the slip." [27]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** Hob was once told he was wrong about something he wasn't, so he comes with his facts ready, to leave nothing to be argued, while good dealings elsewhere keep him from assuming the worst.

**Their response:** "A company blade went out on a POOF order for repair and it's late. I have the order reference and when it left. I'll read them out." [27]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has done right by him before, so he expects the blade to be found, but he is one small smith among many and keeps to the deadline, which is what matters to him.

**Their response:** "I sent a company blade out for repair through POOF, and it hasn't come back. It's needed before they march. Can you find it?" [24]

## Access

**Experience:** Iron the company ordered for my forge is being held at the depot. I'm the company's contact, but the account is in another person's name, and I can't get it released.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** Having been told before that he was mistaken, and expecting nothing from TLC, Hob says only what he needs and holds back the part that might be used against him.

**Their response:** "Iron for the company is being held at the depot. I need it released." [14]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has always been treated as a customer worth the trouble, so he says plainly where he stands on the account and asks what is needed.

**Their response:** "A company order of iron is being held at the depot. I'm the company's contact, not the account holder. What do you need to release it?" [26]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** He knows the account is in someone else's name at the company, that a contact like him can ask about an order, and that the holder can say yes to a release. He doesn't think about what proof TLC wants from the holder.

**How their baggage sits with them:** He has found TLC fair, so he lays out how the account stands and asks how the holder can settle it.

**Their response:** "Iron the company ordered is being held at the depot. The account's in someone else's name; I'm only the contact. Can they approve it, and how?" [26]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** Hob was once told he was mistaken when he wasn't, so he gives the account position before he is asked, to keep the call from turning on it, and he asks for the next step straight away.

**Their response:** "The company's iron is being held at the depot. I'm the contact on the account, not the holder, so tell me who you need to speak to." [27]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has helped him before, so he asks outright, but he has been treated as a small account elsewhere and expects to push for it, so he names the day.

**Their response:** "The company's iron is being held at the depot. I want it released today. Can you do that?" [18]
