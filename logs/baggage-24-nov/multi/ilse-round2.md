# Ilse Harrow, round 2

## Tracking

**Experience:** A delivery for her shop has not arrived when she expected it, and she wants to know where it is.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** A callback she was once promised never came and the suppliers she has dealt with let her down too, so she keeps it short, asks for a direct number up front, and has no idea how any of this works.

"I have a delivery for my shop that hasn't arrived. All I need is where it is right now. If we get cut off, what number can I reach you on?" [31]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** Support has kept its word to her and her suppliers have too, so she calls the way she runs the shop, calmly and prepared, and expects it to be a quick lookup.

"Hello. I'm calling about a delivery to my shop that's later than I expected. I have the reference here whenever you want it. Could you tell me where it is now?" [31]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** She has the order paperwork with the reference and the delivery date she was quoted, and she knows deliveries can be looked up. She thinks the driver could not get to her door because the street outside is dug up, but she would not think to know whether any attempt was actually logged.

**How their baggage sits with them:** Support has kept its word to her and her suppliers have too, so she arrives prepared and offers her own best guess about what went wrong.

"Hello. A delivery for my shop is overdue, and I think the driver couldn't reach the door with the street dug up outside. I have the reference ready. Can you see whether it was tried, and where it is now?" [40]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** The other companies she buys from tell her what she needs to know without being asked, so she has a clear idea of what good looks like, and one promise from TLC that was not kept has made her ask for the real record rather than a guess.

"I'm calling about a delivery to my shop that hasn't come. I have the reference and the date I was given. Please look at the actual record, not just the estimate, and tell me where it is." [37]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has come through for her before, so she assumes it can find anything, but other suppliers have left her chasing, so she doesn't expect to be thanked or listened to for long and gets straight to what she needs.

"Hello. A delivery for my shop hasn't arrived. I don't have a reference in front of me, but you should be able to find it under the shop's name. Where is it, and when will it get here?" [38]

## Access

**Experience:** Her husband Aldo keeps the books for the shop and needs to get into its account, but she is the only person on it.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** A callback she was once promised never came and other companies have shut her out before, so she says exactly what is wrong in order, guesses at the fix, and asks for a number to call back on.

"My husband keeps the books for my shop and he can't get into our account. I'm the only person on it. Can he be added? And what number can I reach you on if we're cut off?" [37]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** Support has kept its word to her and so have the other companies she deals with, so she asks plainly for what she wants and has her side ready.

"Hello. My husband keeps the books for my shop and needs to see our account. I'm the only contact on it, so I'd like to add him. I can verify myself now, and he can come on the line if you need him." [43]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** She knows she is the only contact on the account, that adding someone means proving she is the account holder, and that Aldo needs to see invoices but should not be able to change anything. She would not think to know what the add-a-person process is actually called or what it asks for.

**How their baggage sits with them:** Support has kept its word to her and so have the other companies she deals with, so she arrives knowing exactly what she wants for Aldo and nothing more.

"Hello. My husband keeps the books for my shop and needs to see the invoices, but not change anything. I'm the only contact on the account, so I'd like to add him with viewing only. I can verify myself now." [40]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** The other companies she buys from have always sorted out account matters quickly, so she knows how it should go, and a callback from TLC that never happened makes her want it done while she is on the line.

"I'm calling about getting my husband access to my shop's account. He keeps the books and only needs to see invoices. I'm the only contact on it, and I can verify myself. Can you do that while I'm on the line?" [41]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has been good to her, so she takes it for granted that this is easily fixed, but other companies have made her feel like a small account, so she states it flatly and does not explain more than she has to.

"Hello. My husband keeps the books for my shop and can't get into our account. I'm the only one on it. I'd like him to have access, and I'm sure you can sort that out today." [36]
