# Wren Ashby, round 2

## Tracking

**Experience:** Wren places the ingredient orders for the alchemists' guild workshop, and one order she was expecting this week has not arrived, so she wants to know where it is.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** She has been let down by firms before and expects to be sent from place to place, so she apologises for being on the line at all and offers to explain, as she would to anyone she thinks may not be the right person.

**Their response:** "Oh, hello, sorry. I'm not sure I'm even in the right place for this. I order ingredients for the alchemists' workshop, and one of ours hasn't turned up. I haven't really looked into where it might be, so I don't know what you'd need from me. Should I just explain it, or is there someone else I should ask?" [59]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** She has always found things sorted out when she asks, and she thinks the best of people, so she says it plainly and apologises only for the trouble.

**Their response:** "Hello, thanks. I place the ingredient orders for the alchemists' workshop, and one that was due this week hasn't come. It showed as dispatched, and I've got the order reference in front of me. I checked it twice. Could you tell me where it is now? Sorry, I expect it's just on the road somewhere." [55]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** she knows the order reference, that it showed as dispatched, and that she has looked at the workshop door and the guild hall without finding it; she thinks a quiet spell after dispatch means it is stuck at the depot end, which she is not certain of, and she would not think to ask whether it was split or sent by another route.

**How their baggage sits with them:** Good experience everywhere has left her trusting that asking works, and she thinks the best of people, so she gives what she has and says where she is guessing.

**Their response:** "Hello. I place the ingredient orders for the alchemists' workshop, and one hasn't come. It showed as dispatched, and I've checked the workshop door and the guild hall, so it isn't just sitting there. I've got the order reference here. I think it's stuck at the depot end, but I'm guessing. Where does it show now? Sorry, I'm thinking out loud." [61]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** A bad time with TLC once taught her to hand over everything at the start so no one has to ask her twice, and the good experience elsewhere tells her it can be done properly.

**Their response:** "Hello. I place the ingredient orders for the alchemists' workshop, and one that should have come this week hasn't. I'll give you all of it at once, so you don't have to ask. It showed dispatched, I've got the order reference here, and I've checked the workshop and the guild hall. Can you tell me where it is now, please? Sorry, that was fast." [64]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** Because TLC has been good to her she assumes it will be put right, but she has little to go on and does not feel important enough to take up much of anyone's time, so she apologises and asks.

**Their response:** "Hello, thank you. I'm sorry, I'm not very sure how this works. I order ingredients for the alchemists' workshop, and one order hasn't come, and I thought you'd know where it's got to. I haven't got the reference with me, I'm afraid. I didn't want to take up your time, but could you find it, please? Sorry." [57]

## Access

**Experience:** A crate of ingredients for the workshop is waiting at the depot, and Wren has been told only certain people on the guild's account can collect it, so she wants to know whether she is one of them.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** She has been let down by firms before and expects not to be told much, so she hedges and explains where she stands on the account in case it matters.

**Their response:** "Hello, sorry. I'm not sure this is the right place. There's a crate for our workshop at the depot, I think, and somebody said not everyone can collect it. I'm one of a few people on the guild's account, so I don't know if that includes me. I don't really know who decides that. Could you tell me, or should I ask someone else?" [64]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** She has always found that a question to TLC gets answered, and she thinks the best of people, so she asks directly and apologises only for not checking first.

**Their response:** "Hello, thanks. I'm one of the contacts on the alchemists' guild account, and there's a crate for our workshop waiting at the depot. I was told only certain people on the account can collect it. I'd have thought that included me, since I placed the order. Could you check whether I'm allowed, and if not, who is? Sorry, I should've checked first." [62]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** she knows she is one of several contacts on the guild's account, that she placed the order herself, and that the depot wanted a name that matched the account; she thinks placing the order makes her the person who collects it, which may be wrong, and she would not think of who sets the account's permissions.

**How their baggage sits with them:** Good experience everywhere has left her trusting that asking works, and she thinks the best of people, so she gives what she has and says where she is guessing.

**Their response:** "Hello. I'm one of the contacts on the alchemists' guild account, and I placed the order for a crate that's waiting at the depot. They said only certain names on the account can collect it. I'd assumed that included me, since it was my order, but I might be wrong. Could you check who's down to collect it? Sorry, I'm thinking out loud." [63]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** A bad time with TLC once taught her to say everything up front, and the good experience elsewhere tells her a clear answer is possible, so she lays out her case and asks for a plain answer.

**Their response:** "Hello. I'm a contact on the alchemists' guild account, and I placed the order for a crate that's waiting at the depot. They said only some people on the account can collect it. I'll give you all of it so you don't have to ask: I think it should be me. Could you check, please, and tell me who is allowed? Sorry, that was a lot." [66]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** Because TLC has been good to her she trusts it can be settled, but she knows little of how it works and does not feel important enough to press, so she asks gently and apologises.

**Their response:** "Hello, thank you. I'm sorry, I don't know much about how this works. There's a crate for our workshop at the depot, and they said only some people can collect it, and I'm not sure if that's me. I'm one of the guild's contacts, I think. I'm sure you can sort out who's allowed. Sorry to bother you." [58]
