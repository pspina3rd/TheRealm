# Ilse Harrow, baggage round 1

## Tracking

**Experience:** Stock she ordered for the shop was due to reach her last week and hasn't come. She has customers waiting on some of it and wants to know where it is.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** Having been let down before and thinking little of what support can do, Ilse, who hates wasting time, asks for the smallest useful thing and doesn't set out the whole case.

**Their response:** "Hello. My shop's delivery was due last week and it hasn't come. Can you tell me where it is, or whether it's still on its way?" [26]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** Years of dealing well with TLC and other firms have taught Ilse, who keeps her word to customers, to expect a quick answer, so she opens with everything the agent needs to look it up.

**Their response:** "Hello. I'm calling about the shop's delivery, due at the end of last week and still not here. I have the order reference in front of me. Could you pull up where it's got to and who's carrying it? I've customers waiting on some of it." [46]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** She has the order reference from her supplier's paperwork and knows the delivery is carried by TLC. She knows the street outside has been dug up, so a driver may not have reached the door, and she has already asked Greta next door. She would not think to have the driver's name or what the delivery is filed under.

**How their baggage sits with them:** Being organised and used to things going right, Ilse does her own checking first and then hands the agent a likely reason and a precise question.

**Their response:** "Hello. A delivery for my shop was due last week. It isn't with my neighbour, and the street outside has been dug up, so the driver may not have reached us. I have the order reference. Can you see where it was left or held?" [46]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** Because TLC once left her waiting and other firms have kept her informed, Ilse, who values a promise kept, knows exactly what she wants to be told and makes sure the call doesn't leave her waiting again.

**Their response:** "Hello. I'm calling about a delivery for my shop, due last week, order reference in front of me. I'd like to know where it is and what happens next. If you need to check something, I'll hold while you do." [40]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** Ilse trusts TLC to be able to fix this, but other firms have taught her that her small shop comes last, so she keeps the call short and ready to do whatever is asked.

**Their response:** "Hello, I'll be quick. My shop's delivery was due last week and hasn't come. Can you find it and get it to us? Tell me what you need from me and I'll fetch it." [34]

## Access

**Experience:** Her husband Aldo keeps the shop's books and needs to see the orders and invoices, but he can't get into the shop's account. Ilse is the only person on it, so she is calling to get him in.

### Profile A

**How their baggage sits with them:** Past letdowns and little faith in support leave Ilse, who dislikes wasting anyone's time, stating the bare problem and asking only whether it can be done.

**Their response:** "Hello. My husband does the books for my shop and he can't get into our account. I'm the only one on it. Is that something you can do?" [28]

### Profile B

**How their baggage sits with them:** Good dealings with TLC and other firms have made Ilse, a businesslike person, expect this to be routine, so she names what she wants done and offers to prove who she is.

**Their response:** "Hello. I'm the contact on my shop's account, and my husband, who keeps our books, needs access to the order records. Could you add him to the account? I can confirm who I am however you need." [37]

### Profile B, knowing a lot worked out

**What they know going in:** She is the owner and only contact, and expects that she must prove who she is before anyone is added. She knows Aldo only needs to read orders and invoices for the books. She would not think to know what the access options are called or what they allow.

**How their baggage sits with them:** Being organised and used to things going smoothly, Ilse works out in advance what Aldo needs and asks for it in plain terms.

**Their response:** "Hello. I'm the owner and only contact on the shop's account. My husband Aldo keeps our books and needs to see our orders and invoices, but not change anything. Could you give him his own login? I can verify myself." [40]

### Mix C

**How their baggage sits with them:** TLC once left her waiting and other firms have been straightforward, so Ilse, who keeps her word, asks what is needed from her and for something she can point to afterwards.

**Their response:** "Hello. I'm the only contact on my shop's account, and my husband, who does our books, needs to see the orders and invoices. I'd like to know what you need from me, and I'd like a reference for this call." [40]

### Mix D

**How their baggage sits with them:** Ilse trusts TLC to be able to do this, but other firms have taught her that a small shop comes last, so she keeps it short and asks what to send.

**Their response:** "Hello, I'll be quick. My husband keeps the books for my shop and can't get into our account. I'm the only one on it. Can you sort that out? Tell me what to send you." [35]
