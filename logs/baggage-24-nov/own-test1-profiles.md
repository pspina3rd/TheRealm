# Baggage test 1: sixteen responses (24 November)
Profile A: TLC poor, others poor, support little, valued not much, knows little. Profile B: all good, support a lot, valued very, knows a lot. Word counts in brackets.

## Hob Tallis

### Tracking

#### Profile A

- **Experience:** The iron the company ordered for the forge was due and hasn't turned up, and he is ringing as the company's contact to find out where it is.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has been told he was mistaken when he wasn't, so after poor dealings with TLC and others he gives only the exact fact about the iron and keeps back the rest.
- **Their response:** "Iron for the company's forge. It was due and it hasn't come. I need to know where it is." [19]

#### Profile B

- **Experience:** The iron the company ordered for the forge was due and hasn't turned up, and he is ringing as the company's contact to find out where it is.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has found TLC straight with him, and a man who wants straight answers gives the account and the facts at once because he expects them to be used.
- **Their response:** "It's the company's account, I'm their smith. A load of iron for the forge was due and it hasn't come. Can you tell me where it is?" [27]

### Access

#### Profile A

- **Experience:** He needs to see which of the company's blades are out for repair, but his name isn't on the account and the quartermaster usually sees to it.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has been brushed off before for not being the account holder, so he says what he needs and leaves out the reasons.
- **Their response:** "I need to see which of the company's blades are out for repair. The quartermaster usually sees to it." [19]

#### Profile B

- **Experience:** He needs to see which of the company's blades are out for repair, but his name isn't on the account and the quartermaster usually sees to it.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has had fair dealings and knows how a company account works, so he says plainly whose account it is and what he is asking for.
- **Their response:** "I'm the company's smith, ringing for them. I need to see which blades they have out for repair. My name isn't on the account, the quartermaster's is. Can you add me?" [31]

## Wren Ashby

### Tracking

#### Profile A

- **Experience:** An ingredients order for the guild workshop hasn't arrived, and she placed it and has checked the date.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren checks everything twice and would rather look silly than get it wrong, so she apologises for asking and hedges what she is sure of, and being open she explains more than she needs to.
- **Their response:** "Hi, sorry to bother you. I'm Wren, from the alchemists' guild. I place the ingredient orders for the workshop, and one hasn't come. I've checked the date twice, so I think it's late, but I might have it wrong. I'd like to know where it is, if that's all right." [50]

#### Profile B

- **Experience:** An ingredients order for the guild workshop hasn't arrived, and she placed it and has checked the date.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren believes most people are doing their best, so she talks to the agent as someone who will help, and being open and unsure she still thinks aloud about whether it is late.
- **Their response:** "Hello, this is Wren, from the alchemists' guild. I place the ingredient orders for the workshop, and one hasn't arrived. I've got the order here, and I think it should have come by now, though I could be wrong. Could you tell me where it is? We'd like to start on it soon, so I'm hoping it's just on its way." [61]

### Access

#### Profile A

- **Experience:** A newer journeyman at the workshop needs to place ingredient orders on the guild's account and can't get in; Wren is one of its many contacts but isn't sure she can add anyone.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren is first to wonder whether she is the wrong person to ask, so she apologises, offers her guess about her own part, and fills the gap with explanation.
- **Their response:** "Hi, sorry to bother you. I'm Wren, from the alchemists' guild. One of the other journeymen can't get into our ingredient orders. I'm a contact on the account, I think, so I wondered if I might be allowed to sort it, but I may have that wrong. I can explain more, if that's useful." [54]

#### Profile B

- **Experience:** A newer journeyman at the workshop needs to place ingredient orders on the guild's account and can't get in; Wren is one of its many contacts but isn't sure she can add anyone.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren trusts that people help each other, so she says what the workshop needs, what she thinks she can do, and asks how, without bracing for a no.
- **Their response:** "Hello, this is Wren, from the alchemists' guild. One of our newer journeymen needs to place ingredient orders for the workshop, and she can't get into the account yet. I'm one of the contacts on it, so I think I can add her, but I'd like you to tell me how. Could you help with that?" [56]

## Ilse Harrow

### Tracking

#### Profile A

- **Experience:** An order of stock for her shop hasn't arrived; she is the shop's only contact and has the order number to hand.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Ilse has run the shop twenty years and doesn't waste anyone's time, so she gives the problem briefly and offers the order number only if it would help.
- **Their response:** "An order for my shop hasn't arrived, and it should have by now. I can give you the order number, if that helps." [23]

#### Profile B

- **Experience:** An order of stock for her shop hasn't arrived; she is the shop's only contact and has the order number to hand.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Ilse keeps her word and expects others to keep theirs, so she gives her name, the problem and the order number up front, in the order the agent will want them.
- **Their response:** "Hello, this is Ilse Harrow, from the shop. An order of ours hasn't arrived. It was due, and I have the order number here. Can you tell me where it is?" [31]

### Access

#### Profile A

- **Experience:** Her husband Aldo, who keeps the shop's books, can't get into the shop's account; she is the only name on it.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Ilse is organised and businesslike, and a long-standing shopkeeper who has little time for a poor call, so she states the facts in a few plain lines and does not ask for anything yet.
- **Their response:** "My husband keeps the books for the shop, and he can't get into our account. I'm the only name on it." [21]

#### Profile B

- **Experience:** Her husband Aldo, who keeps the shop's books, can't get into the shop's account; she is the only name on it.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Ilse runs the shop on her word and on tidy books, so she names who needs access and why, says she is the only name on it, and asks for the one thing that fixes it.
- **Their response:** "Hello, this is Ilse Harrow, from the shop. My husband Aldo keeps our books, and he needs to see the shop's account. I'm the only name on it. Can you add him?" [32]

- **Their response:** "Someone in my party can't get into our account. He joined in the spring and it still isn't sorted. I set it up myself." [24]

#### Profile B

- **Experience:** Pell, who joined the party in the spring, still can't get into the party's account with TLC, though she set it up herself.
