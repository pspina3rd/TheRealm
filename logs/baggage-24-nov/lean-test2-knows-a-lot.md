# Lean test 2 — profile: TLC good, others good, support can do a lot, very valued, knows a lot

Opening: "Thank you for contacting TLC, this is [Name]. How may I serve you?"

## Hob Tallis

### Tracking
1. **Experience:** A delivery of bar iron for the forge was due and has not come, and he needs it for the company's work.
2. **What they know going in:** He knows exactly what he ordered, that it is on the company's account, and roughly when it should have come. He would not think to have anything about how the delivery is routed or who last handled it.
3. **How their baggage sits with them:** A man who values exactness has found this support exact in return, so he gives the plain facts in order and expects one call to be enough.
4. **Their response:** "Hello. Calling for the company's account. A delivery of bar iron for the forge hasn't come. Where is it?" [19 words]

### Access
1. **Experience:** One of the company's sergeants needs to be able to collect parcels under the account when Hob is at the forge.
2. **What they know going in:** He knows the account is the company's, that he is its contact, and that only named people can collect. He would not think to have what the agent needs in order to add someone.
3. **How their baggage sits with them:** Having found arrangements like this settled quickly before, he asks for the change plainly, as one more job done right.
4. **Their response:** "Hello. I'm the contact on the company's account. One of our sergeants needs to be able to collect parcels when I'm at the forge. Can you add him?" [28 words]

## Wren Ashby

### Tracking

3. **How their baggage sits with them:** Because she has been well looked after before, she asks the question she is unsure of without worry, expecting it to be sorted.
4. **Their response:** "Hi, thank you. I'm one of the contacts on the guild's account, for the workshop ingredient orders. We have a new journeyman starting, and she'll need to see and place orders too. I'm not sure who normally adds people, but I thought I'd ask here. Could you help with that? I have her name." [54 words]
