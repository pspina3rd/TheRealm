# Rerun of test 3: mixes C and D, four customers, two categories
Agent's opening: "Thank you for contacting TLC, this is [Name]. How may I serve you?"

## Hob

### Hob, Tracking

#### Mix C

- **Experience:** The iron he needs for the forge hasn't come, and he can't tell where it is.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob is exact about metal and has learned that being exact is what keeps him from being talked round, so he comes with the order number and date ready and asks his one question.
- **Their response:** "The iron for my forge hasn't come, and I can't tell where it is. I have the order number and the date it was sent. Can you find it?" [29 words]

#### Mix D

- **Experience:** The iron he needs for the forge hasn't come, and he can't tell where it is.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has always had straight dealing from TLC and has had little patience for anyone else's, so he says what is wrong in a few words and trusts the looking-up to them, though he doesn't have the order details to hand.
- **Their response:** "I'm waiting on iron for the forge and it hasn't come. I can't tell where it is. Can you look it up for me?" [24 words]

### Hob, Access

#### Mix C

- **Experience:** He calls for the company, and there is an order on its account that someone else put in, which he can't open.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** As the company's contact and not the account holder, Hob knows exactly what he is and isn't entitled to, so he names the account and the problem plainly and keeps it short because he expects the answer to depend on the paperwork.
- **Their response:** "I'm calling for the company. There's an order on its account that someone else put in, and I can't open it. I have the account name and the order date here." [31 words]

#### Mix D

- **Experience:** He calls for the company, and there is an order on its account that someone else put in, which he can't open.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has always found TLC straight with him, but he rings for a company whose account he doesn't know the workings of, so he states the problem and asks what to do rather than guess.
- **Their response:** "I'm calling for the company. Someone else put an order on its account and I can't open it. Can you tell me what to do?" [25 words]

## Wren

### Wren, Tracking

#### Mix C

- **Experience:** An ingredients order for the workshop hasn't arrived, and she can't find out where it is.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren checks everything twice, so she arrives with the order details ready, and being apologetic and careful by nature she asks whether this is the right person before she says much more.
- **Their response:** "Hi, sorry, I think this is the right line? I'm calling about an ingredients order for the alchemists' workshop. It hasn't arrived, and I can't find out where it is. I've got the order number and the date, so I can read them out. I'm not sure if you're the one who handles this, but I hope so." [58 words]

#### Mix D

- **Experience:** An ingredients order for the workshop hasn't arrived, and she can't find out where it is.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren believes most people are doing their best and has found TLC kind to her, so she talks easily and apologises for what she doesn't have, trusting the person on the line to look it up.
- **Their response:** "Oh, hi, thanks. We're waiting on an ingredients order for the workshop and it hasn't arrived, and I can't find out where it is. I don't have the order number to hand, I'm sorry, but I'm sure you can look it up. Could you help me with that?" [48 words]

### Wren, Access

#### Mix C

- **Experience:** She is one of many contacts on the guild's account, and there is an order a colleague placed that she needs but can't open.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren hates letting the workshop down and checks everything twice, so she has the account details ready, and she is careful to ask whether this is the right desk before she explains the rest.
- **Their response:** "Hi, sorry to bother you. I'm one of the contacts on the guild's account. A colleague placed an order that I need, and I can't open it. I have the account name and the date it went in, if that helps. I'm not sure if you're the right desk for this, though." [52 words]

#### Mix D

- **Experience:** She is one of many contacts on the guild's account, and there is an order a colleague placed that she needs but can't open.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren would rather ask than get it wrong and has found TLC good to deal with, so she explains her situation in full and says kindly what she doesn't know, hoping not to take up the person's time.
- **Their response:** "Hello, sorry to bother you. I'm one of the people on the guild's account, and there's an order a colleague placed that I need but I can't open. I'm not sure what you need from me. Can you help me with it?" [42 words]

## Ilse

### Ilse, Tracking

#### Mix C

- **Experience:** A stock order for her shop hasn't arrived, and she wants to know where it is.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Ilse keeps her word to customers and values not wasting anyone's time, so she comes with everything an agent could want and leaves little room for a second call.
- **Their response:** "Good morning. I have a stock order for the shop that hasn't arrived, and I want to know where it is. I have the order number, the date it was placed and the delivery address ready, so I can give you whatever you need." [44 words]

#### Mix D

- **Experience:** A stock order for her shop hasn't arrived, and she wants to know where it is.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Ilse has found TLC dependable, and having built the shop herself she expects a plain request to be met, so she asks briefly and offers to dig out whatever is needed.
- **Their response:** "Good morning. A stock order for my shop hasn't arrived, and I'd like to know where it is. I'm sure it's a quick thing to look up. Tell me what you need from me and I'll find it." [38 words]

### Ilse, Access

#### Mix C

- **Experience:** She is the only contact on the shop's account, and her husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Ilse runs the shop on her own account and her word, so she arrives with the account details in hand and asks only whether it can be done, which saves both of them time.
- **Their response:** "Good morning. I'm the only contact on the shop's account, and my husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices. I have the account details here. Can you tell me whether that's something you can set up?" [39 words]

#### Mix D

- **Experience:** She is the only contact on the shop's account, and her husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Ilse has always found TLC good to deal with, and she would rather ask the right question than make a mess of the books, so she says the situation clearly and asks what to do.
- **Their response:** "Good morning. I'm the only contact on the shop's account, and my husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices. Can you add him, or tell me what we need to do? I'd rather ask than guess." [39 words]

## Sella

### Sella, Tracking

#### Mix C

- **Experience:** Something that was sent for her party hasn't arrived, and she wants to know where it is.

### Sella, Access

#### Mix C

#### Mix D

- **Experience:** Something in the party's shared bag can be reached by her but not by Pell.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Sella is sure about what she sees and unsure about why, and having found TLC dependable she hands over the problem as it is and asks them to sort it.
- **Their response:** "Hi. There's something in our party's shared bag that I can get at and Pell can't. He's in my party. I'm not sure why. Can you sort that out? Tell me what you need from me." [36 words]
