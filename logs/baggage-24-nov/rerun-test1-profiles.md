# Baggage test 1: the two profiles, rerun (24 November)

## Hob Tallis

### Tracking

#### Profile A

1. **Experience:** The iron he needs for the forge hasn't come, and he can't tell where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Having been told before that he was mistaken when he wasn't, Hob gives only the plain fact and nothing more, so there is nothing in it anyone can say he got wrong.
3. **Their response:** "The iron for my forge hasn't come, and I can't tell where it is." [14]

#### Profile B

1. **Experience:** The iron he needs for the forge hasn't come, and he can't tell where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has always been taken at his word, so he gives the facts in his usual order and has the number ready, because a smith expects the work to go straight through.
3. **Their response:** "The iron I ordered for the forge hasn't come, and I can't tell where it is. I have the order number ready." [22]

### Access

#### Profile A

1. **Experience:** He calls for the company, and there is an order on its account that someone else put in, which he can't open.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob, ringing for a company whose account isn't his, keeps to what he can prove and leaves out anything he is not sure of.
3. **Their response:** "I'm ringing for the company. There's an order on its account that someone else put in. I can't open it." [20]

#### Profile B

1. **Experience:** He calls for the company, and there is an order on its account that someone else put in, which he can't open.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob is used to dealing for the company and being believed, so he says exactly whose account it is and whose order, and offers what he has.
3. **Their response:** "I'm ringing as the company's contact. An order on the account was put in by someone else, and I can't open it. I have the account name here." [28]

## Wren Ashby

### Tracking

#### Profile A

1. **Experience:** An ingredients order for the workshop hasn't arrived, and she can't find out where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren apologises before she has said anything, because she was passed from person to person once and now expects to be sent on again; so she asks whether this is the right place and explains how hard she looked.
3. **Their response:** "Hi, sorry, I'm not sure if you're the right person for this. It's an ingredients order for the guild workshop, and it hasn't come, and I can't find out where it is. I've looked everywhere I can think of. Is it all right to start with you?" [47]

#### Profile B

1. **Experience:** An ingredients order for the workshop hasn't arrived, and she can't find out where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren checks everything twice and has been looked after before, so she relaxes, introduces herself, and gives the details she has checked.
3. **Their response:** "Hi, thanks for picking up. I'm Wren, from the alchemists' guild workshop. Our ingredients order hasn't arrived, and I can't find out where it is. I have the order number and the date it went in, and I checked them twice, so I think I've got them right. I just can't see where it's gone. Could you look it up?" [60]

### Access

#### Profile A

1. **Experience:** She is one of many contacts on the guild's account, and there is an order a colleague placed that she needs but can't open.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** As one of many on the guild's account, Wren has never been sure whose job anything is, so she hedges, blames herself first, and asks whether this is something the agent can do.
3. **Their response:** "Hi, sorry, I'm not sure this is the right place. I'm one of the contacts on the guild's account, there are quite a lot of us, and a colleague put in an order that I need, but I can't open it. Is this something you can sort out, or should I be asking someone else?" [55]

#### Profile B

1. **Experience:** She is one of many contacts on the guild's account, and there is an order a colleague placed that she needs but can't open.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren knows how the guild's account is set up and has been well helped before, so she explains it in order and offers what the agent will need.
3. **Their response:** "Hi, it's Wren, from the alchemists' guild. I'm one of the contacts on the guild's account, and a colleague placed an order that I need to see, but it won't open for me. I can give you the account name and my colleague's name, if that helps. I think it's probably a permissions thing, but I'd rather you check than have me guess." [63]

## Ilse Harrow

### Tracking

#### Profile A

1. **Experience:** A stock order for her shop hasn't arrived, and she wants to know where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** After twenty years of running a shop, Ilse is used to chasing things herself, so she keeps it brief, says what she has and hasn't got, and asks to stay on while it is looked into.
3. **Their response:** "Good morning. A stock order for my shop hasn't arrived, and I need to know where it is. Tell me what you need from me. I'd rather stay on the line while you look, if that's all right." [38]

#### Profile B

1. **Experience:** A stock order for her shop hasn't arrived, and she wants to know where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Twenty years of keeping her word to customers, and mostly getting the same back from suppliers, lets Ilse give her details at once and be easy about being rung back.
3. **Their response:** "Good morning. A stock order for my shop hasn't arrived. I have the order number and the date it went in, so I can give you both now. If it's easier for you to look into it and ring me back, that's fine with me." [45]

### Access

#### Profile A

1. **Experience:** She is the only contact on the shop's account, and her husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Ilse built the shop herself and has never shared its account, so she describes the plain need and asks what is required, preferring to stay on while it is done.
3. **Their response:** "Good morning. I'm the only contact on the shop's account, and my husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices. I haven't tried to arrange that before, so I'd like to know what you need from me. I'll stay on the line while we do it." [48]

#### Profile B

1. **Experience:** She is the only contact on the shop's account, and her husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Ilse has run the shop's account for years without trouble, so she says what she wants done, and has what she needs about her husband to hand.
3. **Their response:** "Good morning. I'm the only contact on the shop's account, and my husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices. I'd like to add him to the account. I can give you his name and details now, and you can tell me what else you need." [48]

## Sella Brandt

### Tracking

#### Profile A

1. **Experience:** Something that was sent for her party hasn't arrived, and she wants to know where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Three years of leading a party where things go wrong has taught Sella to ask for little and see what comes, so she gives the bare fact and waits to be asked.
3. **Their response:** "Something that was sent for my party hasn't turned up. I want to know where it is. Ask me what you need. I'd like to get it sorted today." [29]

#### Profile B

1. **Experience:** Something that was sent for her party hasn't arrived, and she wants to know where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Sella has found that what her party depends on usually turns up, so she is plain and quick about it, with the details to hand and a little dry about the delay.
3. **Their response:** "Hi. Something that was sent for my party hasn't arrived. I've got the details here, so tell me what you need and I'll read it out. It should have been with us by now, so I'd like to find out where it's got to." [44]

### Access

#### Profile A

1. **Experience:** Something in the party's shared bag can be reached by her but not by Pell.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Sella is used to sorting out her party's problems herself and keeps what she says to the fact and what she wants, leaving the agent to ask the rest.
3. **Their response:** "There's something in our party's shared bag that I can get to and one of my party can't. I'd like that fixed. I don't know how it works on your end, so you'll have to tell me what you need from me." [42]

#### Profile B

1. **Experience:** Something in the party's shared bag can be reached by her but not by Pell.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Sella expects a plain problem to get a plain fix, so she says which of the party can't reach it and what she wants, and tells the agent to ask.
3. **Their response:** "Hi. Something in our party's shared bag opens for me but not for Pell, who's in my party. I can tell you which item and who's on the account, so go ahead and ask. I'd like Pell able to get at it today." [43]
