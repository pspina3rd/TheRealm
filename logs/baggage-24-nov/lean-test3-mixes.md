# Lean test 3: the two mixes
Written 24 November. Four customers, two categories, two mixes: 16 responses. Each customer's experience is the same under both mixes. Word counts are counted from the text.

## Hob Tallis

### Tracking

**Experience (both mixes):** A crate of iron bar the company ordered for the forge was due and has not come, and he is the one the company expects to chase it.

#### Mix C

- **Experience:** A crate of iron bar the company ordered for the forge was due and has not come, and he is the one the company expects to chase it.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has dealt with TLC for the company before and was told he was mistaken when he was not, and he has had fair dealings with other firms, so he comes with every fact checked and leaves nothing for anyone to correct.
- **Their response:** "I'm calling for the company. A crate of iron bar left your depot and the notice stops there. The address is right, I checked it twice. Where is it now?" [30 words]

#### Mix D

- **Experience:** A crate of iron bar the company ordered for the forge was due and has not come, and he is the one the company expects to chase it.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has found TLC straightforward before and has been let down by other firms, and as a man who knows his metal and little else about shipping, he says the plain fact and hands the finding to someone he trusts to do it.
- **Their response:** "I'm calling for the company. A crate of iron bar hasn't come to the forge. Can you tell me where it is?" [22 words]

### Access

**Experience (both mixes):** The company's iron bar is waiting at the depot, and the man the company sent to collect it was turned away at the counter.

#### Mix C

- **Experience:** The company's iron bar is waiting at the depot, and the man the company sent to collect it was turned away at the counter.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob's last dealings with TLC ended with him being told he had it wrong when he had not, so he arrives with exactly what the account allows and sets it down short and firm.
- **Their response:** "I'm calling for the company. Our man went to collect iron bar at your depot and was turned away. He's down as able to collect on the company's account. Why was he refused?" [33 words]

#### Mix D

- **Experience:** The company's iron bar is waiting at the depot, and the man the company sent to collect it was turned away at the counter.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Hob has had good service from TLC before and rough service elsewhere, and being a smith and not a clerk, he reports what happened and expects the people who know the system to put it right.
- **Their response:** "I'm calling for the company. Our man went for the iron bar at your depot and they wouldn't give it to him. I don't know why. Can you sort it out?" [31 words]
