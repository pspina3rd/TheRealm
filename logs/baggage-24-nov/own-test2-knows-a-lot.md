# Own test 2: knows a lot
Profile for all eight: past interactions with TLC good; other companies good; thinks support can do a lot; feels very valued; knows a lot going in.

## Hob Tallis

### Tracking

- **Experience:** Hob rings for the mercenary company. A delivery of iron stock for the forge was sent by POOF and should have reached him by now. It hasn't, and he wants to know where it is.
- **What they know going in:** He knows the order is on the company's account, what it holds in general terms, roughly when it was due, and that he has the order reference to hand. He would not think to know the route or who carried it.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** He is used to things arriving when the supplier says they will, so a late delivery is a gap to close and he is sure someone can close it.
- **Their response:** "I'm waiting on iron stock for the forge. It was sent by POOF and it should be here by now. I need to know where it is. I have the order reference." [32 words]

### Access

- **Experience:** The company's repaired blades are waiting at the depot. Hob made the repairs and rings for the company, but the account is not his, and the quartermaster who normally collects is away.
- **What they know going in:** He knows the account is the company's and not his own, that the quartermaster is the usual collector, and that the blades are his own work and ready. He would not think to know what the depot needs to release them to someone else.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** He has always dealt with the company's account through its proper people, so he states plainly whose it is and expects to be told what it takes.
- **Their response:** "The company's blades are waiting at the depot. I repaired them, but I'm not the account holder. The quartermaster's away. Who else can collect them?" [25 words]

## Wren Ashby

### Tracking

- **Experience:** Wren placed an ingredient order for the guild workshop through POOF. It has not come, and the workshop is running low on one of the things in it.
- **What they know going in:** She knows what was in the order, that she placed it herself on the guild's account, and that she checked it before sending. She would not think to know where it is held up or who handled it.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** She has always found that people helped when she asked, so she asks without bracing for trouble, and her habit of explaining everything has room to run.
- **Their response:** "Hello, I'm a journeyman at the alchemists' guild, calling about an ingredient order for the workshop. It went through POOF on the guild's account, and I checked it twice before it went, but it hasn't come. We're running low on one of the salts, so I'd really like to know where it's got to." [54 words]

### Access

- **Experience:** A delivery is due at the guild workshop this week and Wren will be away when it arrives. She wants someone else she works with to be able to sign for it.
- **What they know going in:** She knows she is one of several contacts on the guild's account, that deliveries are signed for, and who she would want to name. She would not think to know the full list of people already allowed to sign.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** She expects the people at TLC to be as willing to help as they always were, so she gives the reason and the name without apologising for asking.
- **Their response:** "Hello, I'm a journeyman at the alchemists' guild, and I'm one of the contacts on the guild's account. A delivery is coming to the workshop this week and I won't be there when it arrives. I'd like someone else to be able to sign for it. Pip, another journeyman, is who I'd suggest. Can that be done?" [57 words]

## Ilse Harrow

### Tracking

- **Experience:** Ilse's shop is waiting on a stock order sent by POOF from a supplier. It should have arrived and has not.
- **What they know going in:** She knows the order number, what was in the order, who supplied it, and roughly when it was due, because she keeps her own records. She would not think to know how POOF handled it after it left the supplier.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** She has run a shop on the strength of orders arriving, and TLC has been part of that for years, so she calls expecting a short, workable conversation.
- **Their response:** "Good morning. I'm calling about a stock order for my shop. It was sent by POOF and it hasn't arrived, and it should have by now. I'm the only contact on the shop's account, and I have the order number here, so tell me what you need and I'll read it out." [52 words]

### Access

- **Experience:** Ilse will be away from the shop for a few days. She is the only contact on its account, and her husband Aldo, who keeps the books, needs to be able to deal with TLC while she is gone.
- **What they know going in:** She knows the account is hers alone, that Aldo is the person she wants named, and what he would need to do on it. She would not think to know what TLC needs from her to add someone.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** After years of dealing with TLC she assumes there is a plain way to do this, so she asks for the steps and does not defend the request.
- **Their response:** "I run a shop, and I'm the only contact on its account. I'll be away for a few days, and my husband, Aldo, keeps the books. I'd like him added so he can deal with things while I'm gone. What do you need from me?" [45 words]

## Sella Brandt

### Tracking

- **Experience:** Sella ordered salve and bandages for two of her party who are recovering from injuries. They were sent by POOF to the guild hall and have not turned up.
- **What they know going in:** She knows what she ordered and who it is for, that it went to the guild hall, and that it is the party's order through the guild. She would not think to know what happened to it after dispatch.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** She has found TLC dependable when something has gone astray, so she goes straight to the question and trusts the answer will come.
- **Their response:** "I'm calling about an order for my party. Salve and bandages, for two of us who are recovering. It was sent to the guild hall by POOF, and it hasn't turned up. I'd like to know where it is." [39 words]

### Access

- **Experience:** Sella leads an adventuring party, and the party's account is in her name. Pell joined in the spring and now goes to the guild hall for the party, but is not yet allowed to collect the party's deliveries.
- **What they know going in:** She knows the account is hers, who is in the party, and that Pell is the one who goes to the guild hall. She would not think to know what the process for adding a collector is called or involves.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Having had no trouble getting things sorted with TLC before, she treats this as a small job to be done properly and says so in few words.
- **Their response:** "I lead an adventuring party, and our account's in my name. Pell joined us in the spring, and goes to the guild hall for us now. I want Pell to be able to collect our deliveries. How do I get that set up?" [43 words]
