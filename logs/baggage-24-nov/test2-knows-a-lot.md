# Test 2 — knows a lot going in

Profile: past interactions with TLC good · other companies good · thinks support can do a lot · feels very valued · knows a lot going in.

## Hob Tallis

### Tracking
1. **Experience:** The iron he needs for the forge hasn't come, and he can't tell where it is.
2. **What they know going in:** He knows exactly what he ordered, how much, the grade, the day he placed it and the day it was due, and he has the order number to hand. He would not think to know how the iron is routed or who carries it.
3. **How their baggage sits with them:** Years of getting straight answers from TLC have left him expecting one now, the way he expects it of anyone he pays and who pays him.
4. **Their opening:** "Hob Tallis. I ordered bar iron for the forge last week and it was due Monday. It hasn't come and I can't see where it is. I have the order number here."

### Access
1. **Experience:** He calls for the company, and there is an order on its account that someone else put in, which he can't open.
2. **What they know going in:** He knows it is the company's account and not his, that the quartermaster put the order in, and roughly what it should be for. He would not think to know what his own contact on the account is allowed to open.
3. **How their baggage sits with them:** He has always been dealt with plainly by TLC, so he treats this as a plain matter to be put right and sees no reason to expect otherwise.
4. **Their opening:** "Hob Tallis, calling for the company. The quartermaster put an order on our account and I can't open it. I have the order number. I need to see what's in it."
