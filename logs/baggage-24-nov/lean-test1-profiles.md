# Lean test 1: profiles
Four customers, two categories, two baggage profiles: 16 responses. Each response answers the opening "Thank you for contacting TLC, this is [Name]. How may I serve you?" Word counts are in brackets after each.

## Hob Tallis

### Tracking

**Experience (same under both profiles):** The iron bars for the company's forge were due and have not come, and he wants to know where they are.

#### Profile A (poor with TLC, poor elsewhere, support can do little, not much valued, knows little)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** He has been told before that he was wrong when he wasn't, so he carries a dry expectation of being doubted, and stays with what he can stand behind and asks what is needed.
- **Their response:** "I'm ringing for the company. The iron bars for the forge haven't come. I'd like to know where they are. I don't know what you'll need from me." [28 words]

#### Profile B (good with TLC, good elsewhere, support can do a lot, very valued, knows a lot)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** A man who gets straight answers and gives them back, he is easy with support and expects a plain exchange, so he says what he wants and offers the order.
- **Their response:** "Hob Tallis, for the company. The iron bars for the forge are due and haven't come. Can you tell me where they are? I have the order here, so ask me what you need." [34 words]

### Access

**Experience (same under both profiles):** A parcel for the company is waiting at the depot and the quartermaster will collect it, but Hob is not on the account and doesn't know who will be allowed to take it.

#### Profile A (poor with TLC, poor elsewhere, support can do little, not much valued, knows little)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** Having been told he was mistaken when he was not, he keeps to what he knows for certain and says plainly where it stops.
- **Their response:** "I'm ringing for the company. There's a parcel waiting at the depot, and the quartermaster will collect it. I don't know who they'll let take it. Can you say?" [29 words]

#### Profile B (good with TLC, good elsewhere, support can do a lot, very valued, knows a lot)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** He is a man who wants things done right and expects them to be, so he gives the facts in order and asks only for the one confirmation.
- **Their response:** "Hob Tallis, for the company. A parcel is waiting at the depot and the quartermaster will be collecting it. I'm not on the account, he is. I'd like to confirm he'll be allowed to take it." [36 words]

## Wren Ashby

### Tracking

**Experience (same under both profiles):** An ingredients order for the guild workshop should have come by now and hasn't, and she wants to know where it is.

#### Profile A (poor with TLC, poor elsewhere, support can do little, not much valued, knows little)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren assumes people are doing their best, but she is already sorry to take up time, and so she checks first that she is asking the right person.
- **Their response:** "Hi, this is Wren Ashby, from the alchemists' guild. Sorry, I'm not sure this is the right place for it. We have an ingredients order for the workshop that hasn't come, and I'm trying to work out where it's got to. Is that something you can help with, or does it go to someone else?" [55 words]

#### Profile B (good with TLC, good elsewhere, support can do a lot, very valued, knows a lot)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** Being kind herself and used to kindness back, she settles into explaining and offers more detail than she needs, as she would to a friendly face.
- **Their response:** "Hi, it's Wren Ashby, from the alchemists' guild workshop. We have an ingredients order that should have been with us by now, and I'd like to see where it's got to. I have the order details here, so I can read them out whenever you're ready, and I can tell you what's in it as well." [56 words]

### Access

**Experience (same under both profiles):** The person who usually collects the workshop's orders is away, and an order is waiting at the depot, so she wants to know who is allowed to collect it.

#### Profile A (poor with TLC, poor elsewhere, support can do little, not much valued, knows little)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** Because she checks everything twice and has been bounced around before, she wonders aloud whether this is the right place and whether it can be her.
- **Their response:** "Hello, this is Wren, from the alchemists' guild. Sorry, I'm not sure who I should be asking. Our usual person for collecting orders is away, and there's one waiting for the workshop. I don't know who's allowed to pick it up, or if it can be me. Do you know, or is it someone else?" [55 words]

#### Profile B (good with TLC, good elsewhere, support can do a lot, very valued, knows a lot)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** She trusts that people are doing their best, so she explains the whole situation and her own guess, and is happy to be corrected.
- **Their response:** "Hello, Wren Ashby, from the alchemists' guild. Our usual person for collecting the workshop's orders is away, and there's one waiting at the depot. I'd like to know who else on the account can collect it. I think I can, as I'm one of the contacts on it, but I'd rather check than assume." [54 words]

## Ilse Harrow

### Tracking

**Experience (same under both profiles):** A delivery of stock for her shop was due and hasn't arrived, and she wants to know where it is.

#### Profile A (poor with TLC, poor elsewhere, support can do little, not much valued, knows little)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** A woman who keeps her word and expects others to, Ilse says what she needs briskly and asks for a plain answer now rather than later.
- **Their response:** "Ilse Harrow, I run a shop. A delivery of stock for the shop hasn't arrived. I don't know how this works with you, so tell me what you need from me. If you can't find it, say so now and I'll try something else." [44 words]

#### Profile B (good with TLC, good elsewhere, support can do a lot, very valued, knows a lot)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** Organised and used to dealing with suppliers who deliver, she gets straight to it, with the order number ready, and treats the call as routine.
- **Their response:** "Good morning, Ilse Harrow, from the shop. A delivery of stock for us hasn't arrived and I'd like to know where it is. I have the order number here. I can give it to you now, or read you what was in it, whichever helps." [45 words]

### Access

**Experience (same under both profiles):** Her husband Aldo keeps the shop's books and needs to see its orders and deliveries on the account, and she wants to know whether he can.

#### Profile A (poor with TLC, poor elsewhere, support can do little, not much valued, knows little)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** Not wasting anyone's time, she asks the one question she needs answered and wants to know at once whether it can be done.
- **Their response:** "Ilse Harrow, I run a shop. My husband keeps our books, and he needs to see our orders on the account. I don't know if he can, or how it's done. Is that something you can do, or not?" [39 words]

#### Profile B (good with TLC, good elsewhere, support can do a lot, very valued, knows a lot)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** Used to arranging things with a business she trusts, she describes what she wants done and assumes it is a simple request.
- **Their response:** "Good morning, Ilse Harrow, from the shop. My husband Aldo keeps our books, and he needs to see our orders and deliveries on the account. I'm the only contact on it, so I'd like to add him. What do you need from me?" [43 words]

## Sella Brandt

### Tracking

**Experience (same under both profiles):** The party ordered a new shield for Pell and it hasn't arrived.

#### Profile A (poor with TLC, poor elsewhere, support can do little, not much valued, knows little)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** Her tolerance for things going badly is low, and she has learned to expect little from support, so she asks for what she needs and no more.
- **Their response:** "Sella Brandt. I lead a small party. We ordered a new shield for Pell and it hasn't come. I don't know how you find these things, or whether you can. Can you tell me where it is?" [37 words]

#### Profile B (good with TLC, good elsewhere, support can do a lot, very valued, knows a lot)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** She is practical and sure of what she has seen, and with help she trusts, she gives the facts and lets the agent do the rest.
- **Their response:** "Sella Brandt, I lead a small party. We have a shield on order for Pell and it should be here by now. I have the order here. Can you tell me where it is?" [34 words]

### Access

**Experience (same under both profiles):** Pell, who joined the party in the spring, can't get into the party's locker at the guild hall, and she wants to know who is allowed in.

#### Profile A (poor with TLC, poor elsewhere, support can do little, not much valued, knows little)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** A leader who wants things to just work, she says what has gone wrong in plain words and asks who decides, without hoping much.
- **Their response:** "Sella Brandt. I lead a small party. Pell, who joined us in the spring, can't get into our locker at the guild hall. I don't know who's meant to be able to, or who decides that. Can you tell me?" [40 words]

#### Profile B (good with TLC, good elsewhere, support can do a lot, very valued, knows a lot)

- **How their baggage sits with them:** Used to things being sorted when she asks, she says what she wants done and knows she is the one who can say yes.
- **Their response:** "Sella Brandt, I lead a small party. Pell, who joined us in the spring, can't get into our locker at the guild hall. I'd like Pell added, and I know it needs whoever's on it to say yes. That's me. What do you need from me?" [46 words]
