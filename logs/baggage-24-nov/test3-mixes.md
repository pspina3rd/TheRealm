# Test 3 — Mix C and Mix D, four customers, two categories (16)

Mix C: TLC poor · other companies good · support can do some · somewhat valued · knows a lot.
Mix D: TLC good · other companies poor · support can do a lot · not much valued · knows little.

## Hob Tallis

### Tracking

**Mix C**
1. **Experience:** The iron he needs for the forge hasn't come, and he can't tell where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Being told he was wrong when he wasn't has made him distrust TLC, while the suppliers who simply did what they said have shown him how it should go, so he arrives with the order checked and expects to be doubted.
3. **Their opening:** "I'm calling about iron for my forge. It hasn't come, and your tracking doesn't say where it is. I've checked the order and it's right."

**Mix D**
1. **Experience:** The iron he needs for the forge hasn't come, and he can't tell where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has dealt straight with him before and other firms haven't, so he trusts it to find things, but he is the company's contact and not the one who knows the order, so he asks plainly and does not claim to know more than he does.
3. **Their opening:** "My iron hasn't come, and I can't tell where it is. The forge can't work without it. You've helped before. Can you find it?"

### Access

**Mix C**
1. **Experience:** He calls for the company, and there is an order on its account that someone else put in, which he can't open.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Having been told before that he was mistaken when he wasn't, he expects TLC to say it again, so he comes with the account details already checked and says so first.
3. **Their opening:** "I'm calling for the company. There's an order on its account that someone else put in, and I can't open it. I have the account details here, and they're right."

**Mix D**
1. **Experience:** He calls for the company, and there is an order on its account that someone else put in, which he can't open.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has been straight with him and other firms have not, so he is willing to ask it for a plain answer, and because the account is the company's and not his, he does not pretend to know why he is shut out.
3. **Their opening:** "I'm calling for the company. There's an order on its account that someone else put in, and I can't open it. I don't know why. Can you open it?"

## Wren Ashby

### Tracking

**Mix C**
1. **Experience:** An ingredients order for the workshop hasn't arrived, and she can't find out where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Calls to TLC have been hard going for her before, while the other firms she deals with have been kind and quick, so she does what she always does and checks everything twice, hoping that being thorough will make this one easy.
3. **Their opening:** "Hi, sorry. I order the ingredients for the alchemists' workshop, and one order hasn't arrived, and I can't find out where it is. I've got the order number and the date in front of me, so I can give you whatever you need. I checked it twice."

**Mix D**
1. **Experience:** An ingredients order for the workshop hasn't arrived, and she can't find out where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has always been kind to her and the other firms she's tried have not, so she is relieved to be calling it, but she doesn't have the order's details to hand, so she says what she tried and leaves the rest to them.
3. **Their opening:** "Oh, hello, thank you. Sorry to bother you. An ingredients order for the workshop hasn't come, and I can't find out where it is. I tried the supplier first and couldn't get anywhere, and I don't know what else to check. I thought you'd know."

### Access

**Mix C**
1. **Experience:** She is one of many contacts on the guild's account, and there is an order a colleague placed that she needs but can't open.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** She has been left unsure whether TLC will help, and the other firms she deals with have been easy, so she gives the full picture up front and thinks out loud about why it might be happening, in case that saves anyone time.
3. **Their opening:** "Hi, sorry. I'm one of the contacts on the guild's account, so I thought I'd be able to see an order my colleague placed, but I can't open it. I think it's because she placed it, but I might be wrong. I have the account and the order number here."

**Mix D**
1. **Experience:** She is one of many contacts on the guild's account, and there is an order a colleague placed that she needs but can't open.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has always helped her and other firms haven't, so she expects it can sort this out, but with so many contacts on the account she worries she is a small part of it and says sorry for taking up anyone's time.
3. **Their opening:** "Hello, thank you. I'm one of the people on the guild's account, and a colleague placed an order that I need for the workshop, but I can't open it. I don't know why. I'm sure you'll know what to do. I know there are a lot of us on the account, so I hope it's not a bother."

## Ilse Harrow

### Tracking

**Mix C**
1. **Experience:** A stock order for her shop hasn't arrived, and she wants to know where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** She built the shop herself and keeps her own word, so TLC's poor record irritates her, and the suppliers who do what they say are the standard she measures it against.
3. **Their opening:** "Good morning. A stock order for my shop hasn't arrived, and I'd like to know where it is. I have the order number, the date it was placed and the date it was due, and nothing has moved on the tracking since. I'd appreciate a clear answer on what happens next."

**Mix D**
1. **Experience:** A stock order for her shop hasn't arrived, and she wants to know where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Years of suppliers and a merchant who still owes her have taught her to expect little, and TLC's record is the exception, so she calls it with some confidence and without needing to make a point of it.
3. **Their opening:** "Good morning. A stock order for my shop hasn't arrived, and I'd like to know where it is. I don't have much more than that, I'm afraid. My other suppliers just tell me to be patient. You've always done better than that."

### Access

**Mix C**
1. **Experience:** She is the only contact on the shop's account, and her husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** She has added Aldo to accounts at other suppliers without trouble, so she knows what to ask for, and TLC has let her down before, so she asks for the exact steps instead of assuming it will go smoothly.
3. **Their opening:** "Good morning. I'm the only contact on my shop's account, and my husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices. I've done this with my other suppliers and it was simple, so I'd like to know exactly what you need from me."

**Mix D**
1. **Experience:** She is the only contact on the shop's account, and her husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has done right by her and other companies have not, so she trusts it with this, but she doesn't know how it's done and doesn't expect to be treated as anyone special for asking.
3. **Their opening:** "Good morning. I'm the only contact on the shop's account, and my husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices. I don't know how that's done, so I'm asking you. It seems simple enough, but then so did the last thing I asked another company."

**Mix D**

2. **How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has helped the party before and other places have been worse, so she brings it the problem without trying to explain it, because she can say what she sees but not why.
3. **Their opening:** "I can get at something in our party's shared bag, but Pell can't. I don't know why. You've sorted things out for us before, and everywhere else I've tried has been worse, so I'm calling you."
