# Baggage openings, 24 November

Four customers, two categories, two profiles: 16 entries. The experience is the same under A and B for each customer and category. Profile A is poor, poor, little, not much valued, knows little. Profile B is good, good, a lot, very valued, knows a lot. Everything here is Claude's **[C]** until Phil has read it.

## Hob Tallis

### Tracking

**Profile A**
1. **Experience:** The iron he needs for the forge hasn't come, and he can't tell where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** He has been told he was wrong when he wasn't, by TLC and by others, so he sets out the plain facts first and waits to be doubted, without any fuss about it.
3. **Opening:** "Iron for the forge hasn't come. I don't have a tracking number and I don't know where I'd find one. I'll say what I know, and you tell me if I've got any of it wrong."

**Profile B**
1. **Experience:** The iron he needs for the forge hasn't come, and he can't tell where it is.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** TLC has always given him straight answers, which is what he values most, so he expects one again and sees no need to dress the question up.
3. **Opening:** "Iron for the forge hasn't come. I have the order reference in front of me, so you can look it up. Where is it?"

### Access

**Profile A**
1. **Experience:** He calls for the company, and there is an order on its account that someone else put in, which he can't open.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Being told he was mistaken when he wasn't has taught him to say up front what he is and isn't, and to ask for a plain no rather than a runaround.
3. **Opening:** "I'm the company's contact, not the account holder. There's an order on the account I didn't place and I can't open it. If I'm not allowed, say so plainly."

**Profile B**
1. **Experience:** He calls for the company, and there is an order on its account that someone else put in, which he can't open.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** He has found TLC fair and exact over the years, and a man who pays what he owes expects a fair process to simply work.

### Access

3. **Opening:** "Hi, sorry to take your time. I'm one of lots of people on the guild's account, and there's an order a colleague placed that I can't open. I need it for the workshop. Could you tell me who can give me access, so I only have to ask once?"

**Profile B**

### Access

**Profile B**
1. **Experience:** Something in the party's shared bag can be reached by her but not by Pell.
2. **How their baggage sits with them:** Because things have mostly worked out for her party, she takes this for a small setting that someone there will know about.
3. **Opening:** "Hi. There's something in our shared bag that I can get at and Pell can't. I figure it's a setting somewhere and you'll know which one. Can you take a look?"
