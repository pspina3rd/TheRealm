# Re-run of test 2: knows a lot going in

Profile: past interactions with TLC good; other companies good; thinks support can do a lot; feels very valued; knows a lot going in. Opening: "Thank you for contacting TLC, this is [Name]. How may I serve you?"


## Hob Tallis


### Tracking

- **Experience:** The iron he needs for the forge hasn't come, and he can't tell where it is.
- **What they know going in:** He has the order number and knows the bar size, the weight and the day it was promised, because iron is his trade. He doesn't know where the shipment is or what route it takes.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Years of iron and repairs arriving when promised have made the service for him a tool that works, like a good hammer, so he doesn't think about it and just says what he needs.
- **Their response:** "Hob Tallis. My iron for the forge hasn't come, and I can't tell where it is. I have the order number." [21 words]


### Access

- **Experience:** He calls for the company, and there is an order on its account that someone else put in, which he can't open.
- **What they know going in:** He knows the company's account and the order number, and what the order is for, since the blades and iron are his side of it. He doesn't know how contacts on the account are set up or who can open what.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** The company's dealings with TLC have gone smoothly for as long as he has handled its metalwork, so he treats a problem as a small fault to be fixed and gives the facts and nothing else.
- **Their response:** "Hob Tallis, for the company. Someone else put an order on its account and I can't open it. I have the number." [22 words]


## Wren Ashby


### Tracking

- **Experience:** An ingredients order for the workshop hasn't arrived, and she can't find out where it is.
- **What they know going in:** She knows the order number, what is in it and the date it was due, because she checks everything twice. She doesn't know how the delivery side works or where in it an order can get held up.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Orders for the workshop have always turned up and people have been kind when she asked, so she calls expecting to be helped and feels free to explain everything.
- **Their response:** "Hello, it's Wren Ashby, from the alchemists' guild workshop. Sorry, I'll explain this properly. I ordered ingredients for the workshop and they haven't arrived. They were due on Thursday, and I've checked the confirmation twice, so I'm fairly sure that's right. I have the order number here if that helps." [50 words]


### Access

- **Experience:** She is one of many contacts on the guild's account, and there is an order a colleague placed that she needs but can't open.
- **What they know going in:** She knows the order number, which colleague placed it and that she is one of the guild's contacts, because she places the workshop's orders herself. She wouldn't know who looks after the account or how contacts are set up.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Wren has only ever found people at TLC willing to help, which fits her belief that most people are doing their best, so she explains the whole thing without bracing for trouble.
- **Their response:** "Hello, it's Wren Ashby from the alchemists' guild. I'm one of the contacts on the guild's account, and a colleague placed an order that I need for the workshop, but I can't open it. Sorry, I don't know why. I have the order number and my colleague's name, if you need them." [52 words]


## Ilse Harrow


### Tracking

- **Experience:** A stock order for her shop hasn't arrived, and she wants to know where it is.
- **What they know going in:** She knows the order number, the day she placed it and the day it was due, because she keeps her orders in order. She doesn't know how delivery works behind the scenes, only that her stock should be in her stockroom by now.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Twenty years of running the shop on suppliers who keep their word has taught her to expect the same of TLC, so she opens with facts and no preamble.
- **Their response:** "Good morning, Ilse Harrow. A stock order for my shop was due on Monday and hasn't arrived. I have the order number and the date I placed it. Can you tell me where it is?" [35 words]


### Access

- **Experience:** She is the only contact on the shop's account, and her husband, who keeps the books, needs to see the invoices.
- **What they know going in:** She knows the account is hers alone and that Aldo needs the invoices for the shop's books. She doesn't know how another person gets added to an account.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** Since she built the shop herself and TLC has always been one of the suppliers that kept its word, she brings it a plain request, the way she would to any good supplier.
- **Their response:** "Good morning, Ilse Harrow. I'm the only contact on the shop's account, and my husband keeps the books. He needs to see the invoices. I'd like to know how to give him access. I have the account details here." [39 words]


## Sella Brandt


### Tracking

- **Experience:** Something that was sent for her party hasn't arrived, and she wants to know where it is.
- **What they know going in:** She knows what was sent, who it was for and the day it should have come, and she has the reference to hand. She doesn't know how TLC tracks a delivery or why one stalls.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** For three years deliveries to the party have come when they should, so a missing one is a plain fact to report, and she has little patience for things going badly.
- **Their response:** "Sella Brandt. Something was sent for my party and it hasn't arrived. It was due on Tuesday. I have the reference number here, and I'd like to know where it is." [31 words]


### Access

- **Experience:** Something in the party's shared bag can be reached by her but not by Pell.
- **What they know going in:** She knows what the item is, that she can open it and that Pell can't, and that Pell is a full member of the party. She doesn't know how the bag decides who can reach what.
- **How their baggage sits with them:** The party's dues and dealings through TLC have gone well for three years, so a thing that doesn't work stands out to her, and she says it plainly and wants it fixed.
- **Their response:** "Sella Brandt. My party shares a bag, and there's something in it I can open but Pell can't. I don't know why. Pell is part of the party, so it should work for both of us." [36 words]
