- **Fit:** usual
- **Their opening:** "Hob Tallis. The steel that came for the forge is the wrong kind. It's too soft for blades. I need the right steel sent."

### Tracking

## Wren Ashby

### Shipment
- **Experience:** A delivery of ingredients for the workshop came, and something in it looks wrong to her. She only had a quick look.
- **Fit:** usual
- **Their opening:** "Oh, hello, sorry, this is Wren, from the alchemists' guild workshop. The box that came for us this morning, I think something in it's not right? One of the jars might be the wrong thing, I'm not completely sure. I checked it twice, and I still can't tell, so I'd rather ask than guess."

### Order
- **Experience:** She puts in the ingredient orders for the workshop. She did one yesterday and can't tell whether it went through.
- **Fit:** usual
- **Their opening:** "Hi, sorry, it's Wren, from the alchemists' guild. I do the ingredient buying for our workshop, and I put something through yesterday, but I'm not sure it actually went through. I got a message on the screen, I think, but I can't tell if it was the right sort of message. I'd rather check than have the workshop run short."

### Tracking
- **Experience:** The workshop is expecting a delivery that hasn't turned up, and supplies are getting low.
- **Fit:** usual
- **Their opening:** "Hello, sorry, it's Wren, from the alchemists' guild workshop. We're expecting a delivery and nothing's come. I don't know if I'm just early or if it's late, and we're getting a bit low, so I thought I'd ask rather than wait. Can you tell me where it is?"

### Navigation
- **Experience:** The guild needs something from TLC, and she can't work out which of TLC's services it falls under. She'd like not to be passed around.
- **Fit:** out of their depth
- **Their opening:** "Hi, sorry, this is Wren, from the alchemists' guild. I need something for the workshop, and I've been trying to work out which part of TLC it is, and I keep going round in circles. I'm sure it's obvious, I just can't see it. I'd rather not get sent from person to person if I can help it. Could you tell me who I want?"

### Access
- **Experience:** A new journeyman has started at the workshop and can't see what the rest of them can on TLC's side. Wren isn't sure whether she's allowed to fix that.
- **Fit:** out of their depth
- **Their opening:** "Hi, sorry, it's Wren, from the alchemists' guild. We've got a new journeyman and she can't see what the rest of us see on your side. I think I might be able to sort that out? Or maybe I'm not the right person. Do you know who is?"

### Account
- **Experience:** Someone at the guild said the billing address TLC holds for the guild is old. Wren is only one of the guild's contacts and isn't sure it's hers to change.
- **Fit:** out of their depth
- **Their opening:** "Hello, sorry, it's Wren, from the alchemists' guild. Somebody said the address you send our bills to is an old one, and I don't know if that's true. I'm not even sure it's mine to change, there are a lot of us who ring you. But I thought I should ask before it goes wrong."

### User
- **Experience:** Messages meant for her don't seem to reach her. She wonders whether TLC has her details wrong, or whether the fault is at the guild.
- **Fit:** usual
- **Their opening:** "Hi, sorry, it's Wren Ashby, from the alchemists' guild. Messages from you keep not reaching me, so I wondered if the details you have for me might be wrong. Or it might be something on our side, I honestly don't know. Could you tell me what you have for me?"

### Repair
- **Experience:** Someone at the guild mentioned that something was sent off to be mended. Wren didn't send it and doesn't know what it is.
- **Fit:** barely fits
- **Their opening:** "Hi, sorry, I'm Wren, from the alchemists' guild. This might be the wrong place to ask. Someone said something of ours was sent off to be mended, and I wasn't the one who sent it, so I don't really know what it is. Is that something you'd have a record of? I can go and find out who sent it if not."
