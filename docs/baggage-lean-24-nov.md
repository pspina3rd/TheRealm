# Baggage tests: positions as a lean — 24 November

Phil, 24 November: *"So you've either got wunderkinds who know every answer
or incompetent boobs who know nothing"*. The own-experience prompt
(`docs/baggage-own-experience-24-nov.md`) now says three more things:
- Each position is a lean, not all or nothing.
- What a customer knows going in is what they know *or think they know*
  (rule 49), which may be wrong or out of date.
- How sure they sound and how often they are right comes from their
  certainty and accuracy.

All three tests were run, one Sonnet run each. Results are in
`logs/baggage-24-nov/lean-test1-profiles.md`, `lean-test2-knows-a-lot.md`
and `lean-test3-mixes.md`.

Phil, 24 November: *"I don't know is a valid answer, in some scenarios for
both player and/or customer."*
