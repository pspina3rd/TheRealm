# Author decision log

Running record of Philip Spina's decisions on the story plans. Decision IDs come from the plans
listed in [README.md](README.md). A decision recorded here overrides the plan's working version.
Undecided items remain proposals.

| Status | Meaning                                                 |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Yes    | Approved as written in the plan.                        |
| No     | Rejected. The plan's proposal must not be built.        |
| Held   | Deliberately deferred; the note says what it waits for. |
| Open   | Not yet discussed.                                      |
| Change | Approved with the author's change recorded in the note. |

## Round 3 — Rebuilt opening (5 November 2026)

These replace earlier canon wherever they conflict, including World Bible WB-C16 (Rowan's knees).

| Topic         | Decision                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fern          | He/him. Scouts for the Mossvale watch; the night patrol ends at the stone circle.                                                                                      |
| Rowan         | Current captain of the watch. Childhood best friend of Fern's father; nearly a second father to Fern.                                                                  |
| Fern's father | Bracken. Dead. Rowan believes his own inaction caused the death. Cause and circumstances not yet decided.                                                              |
| Rowan's limit | No bad knees. A skewed sense of duty to the town, and his guilt over Fern's father, keep him from going to Brightwater himself.                                        |
| Lamp lighting | Dropped, along with the lamp order.                                                                                                                                    |
| Opening night | The stones flicker at the patrol's last stop, then the violet stone goes out and the six dim behind Fern; he runs to Rowan. The watch saying: "Seven lit. All's well." |

| Topic          | Decision                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Sleep" breath | Cut from the opening. The felt voice saying "Sleep" is an unearned call to adventure; the stones dimming is enough to send Fern running.                 |
| Stone colours  | As the game draws them: violet (Arcane), blue (Water), cyan (Wind), green (Nature), gold (Lightning), orange (Earth), red (Fire). Violet goes out first. |

| Topic            | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Fern's character | Restless and hungry for excitement in a quiet village, and still grieving Bracken. Both are true at once.                                                                                                                                  |
| Rowan's son      | Hareguy (said "Hair-gway"; "Hair Guy" colloquially). Alive and awake in Brightwater; not a sleeper. Half-elf: his mother, Rowan's wife, was an Elf. His name is the common-tongue form of a traditional name in this land's elvish tongue. |

| Topic        | Decision                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rowan's wife | An Elf. She believed Hareguy's latent elvish abilities would develop, that the stones would play a part, and that this would follow a sign. |

| Topic           | Decision                                                                                                                  |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Elves           | Rare. Most have left the land; the few who remain are often hidden and unseen.                                            |

| Hareguy's name  | He corrects people to "Hair-gway"; Mossvale keeps calling him "Hair Guy". Show this in a scene rather than explaining it. |

## Round 5 — Look of the opening (8 November 2026)

| Topic          | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The stones     | The pack's large boulders, each one different. Like yooperlites under a black light: plain grey rock when out; when lit the rock darkens and glowing patches, veins and flecks of the stone's colour show through. Replaces the crystal idea. |
| Everywhere     | The overworld's stones use the same art as the story scenes.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Hilltop layout | Quasi-deliberate: a ring set round the back of the hilltop long ago, settled slightly unevenly since; open toward the path, violet at the left end and red at the right. The scene stage is 480 x 270 to give the stones room.                |
| Birches        | The pack's own round tree recoloured as a birch, not drawn from scratch.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Cottage        | Rowan's cottage: timber-framed plaster walls, a wooden plank floor, and a stone fireplace with the fire in it. Built from the pack's tiles.                                                                                                   |

| Platforms | The iPhone version of the illustrated scenes waits until the desktop (web) version is settled. |

## Round 7 — The rest of the opening (8 November 2026)

| Topic            | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Morning after    | Nobody else saw the stones go out. Rowan keeps the usual patrols and adds a dedicated watch at the stones every night, dusk to dawn, telling the watch only to watch and report. He keeps what Fern saw to himself and takes the first night at the stones himself. No neighbour cameos here.                                    |
| What is at stake | Not explained in the opening. Fern learns it from the teachers as he tells them why he has come.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| First teacher    | Rowan recommends Kestrel at Windward Cliffs (Wind), the nearest teacher who can take a beginner, and a fit for a scout and Ranger. The map is unchanged, and the player may still choose another teacher first. Dorn comes later.                                                                                                |
| The rune stone   | Rowan gives Fern the old rune stone he found digging the well. Its purpose stays a mystery; it may matter for Weaving.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Bracken's death  | Ten years ago a collapse at Redrock trapped one of Dorn's crews. Bracken, captain of the Mossvale watch, took men to dig them out. Rowan called a retreat; Bracken kept getting others out. Rowan pulled the last miner clear as Bracken pushed him through from inside; Rowan turned to help his friend as the rocks came down. |
| Rowan's guilt    | He took the miner first, when Bracken was right behind. (This replaces "inaction".) Dorn was there, and it surfaces when Fern meets him.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
