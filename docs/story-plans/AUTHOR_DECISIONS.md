# Author decision log

Running record of Philip Spina's decisions on the story plans. Decision IDs come from the plans
listed in [README.md](README.md). A decision recorded here overrides the plan's working version.
Undecided items remain proposals.

| Status | Meaning                                                 |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Yes    | Approved as written in the plan.                        |
| No     | Rejected. The plan's proposal must not be built.        |
| Held   | Deliberately deferred; the note says what it waits for. |
| Open   | Not yet discussed.                                      |
| Change | Approved with the author's change recorded in the note. |

## Round 3 — Rebuilt opening (5 November 2026)

These replace earlier canon wherever they conflict, including World Bible WB-C16 (Rowan's knees).

| Topic         | Decision                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fern          | He/him. Scouts for the Mossvale watch; the night patrol ends at the stone circle.                                                                                      |
| Rowan         | Current captain of the watch. Childhood best friend of Fern's father; nearly a second father to Fern.                                                                  |
| Fern's father | Bracken. Dead. Rowan believes his own inaction caused the death. Cause and circumstances not yet decided.                                                              |
| Rowan's limit | No bad knees. A skewed sense of duty to the town, and his guilt over Fern's father, keep him from going to Brightwater himself.                                        |
| Lamp lighting | Dropped, along with the lamp order.                                                                                                                                    |
| Opening night | The stones flicker at the patrol's last stop, then the violet stone goes out and the six dim behind Fern; he runs to Rowan. The watch saying: "Seven lit. All's well." |

| Topic          | Decision                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Sleep" breath | Cut from the opening. The felt voice saying "Sleep" is an unearned call to adventure; the stones dimming is enough to send Fern running.                 |
| Stone colours  | As the game draws them: violet (Arcane), blue (Water), cyan (Wind), green (Nature), gold (Lightning), orange (Earth), red (Fire). Violet goes out first. |

| Topic            | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Fern's character | Restless and hungry for excitement in a quiet village, and still grieving Bracken. Both are true at once.                                                                                                                                  |
| Rowan's son      | Hareguy (said "Hair-gway"; "Hair Guy" colloquially). Alive and awake in Brightwater; not a sleeper. Half-elf: his mother, Rowan's wife, was an Elf. His name is the common-tongue form of a traditional name in this land's elvish tongue. |
| The call         | Working direction: a letter asks Rowan's son to come home and help with the stones. Rowan's duty will not let him leave Mossvale in its hour of need, so Fern carries the letter and becomes the unexpected hero.                          |

| Topic        | Decision                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rowan's wife | An Elf. She believed Hareguy's latent elvish abilities would develop, that the stones would play a part, and that this would follow a sign. |
| The sign     | The unchanging stones dimming is that sign, which is why the letter calls Hareguy home.                                                     |

| Topic              | Decision                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Elves              | Rare. Most have left the land; the few who remain are often hidden and unseen.                                                    |
| Elvish language    | Not used in the story's text. Only names carry it (Hareguy's is the common-tongue form of his elvish name).                       |
| Hareguy's name     | He corrects people to "Hair-gway"; Mossvale keeps calling him "Hair Guy". Show this in a scene rather than explaining it.         |
| Rowan and the sign | Rowan never quite believed his wife's prediction. The stones dimming is the moment he has to, and it is why he writes to Hareguy. |
