# Baggage test — 24 November

Phil, 24 November: *"Sure let's test them with those"* — the five kinds of
baggage, placed by position only (`docs/card-rules.md`, 48–51). Four
customers (Hob, Wren, Ilse, and Sella on Sheet Y with her life from the
record), two categories (Tracking; Access), two opposite profiles (A: poor,
poor, little, not much, little; B: good, good, a lot, very, a lot): 16
openings, written by one Sonnet subagent and read by me. Same experience
under both profiles, so only the baggage differs. Openings in
`logs/baggage-24-nov/openings.md`.

Five failed my read and were rewritten: two of Wren's under Profile A ran
her down (*"it usually is for me"*; assuming the fault is hers); Wren
*"hoped I'd get you"*, not knowing which agent she had; Hob's regional *"I've
no…"* and *"I've the…"*; Sella's *"so I figured I'd start with you"*.

## What it shows

**The same person reads differently with different baggage.** All four,
in both categories, open differently under A and B.

**Under poor baggage, the same position reads differently in each
person.** Each brings something different from their own sheet:
- Hob sets out the facts and invites being corrected: *"You tell me if I've
  got any of it wrong."*
- Wren asks not to be passed around: *"Could you tell me who is, so I'm not
  passed around?"*
- Ilse wants something to hold TLC to: *"Before we start, I'd like a name
  and a reference for this call."*
- Sella expects a rule nobody told her about: *"Probably a rule somewhere.
  There usually is."*

**Under good baggage, three of the four converge.** Hob, Wren and Ilse all
lead with having the order number, and ask for a quick look-up. Only
Sella's reads as hers (*"You've never lost anything of ours before"*). The
cause is the fifth kind: *knows a lot going in* came out as the same thing
for all three, having the reference to hand.
