# Baggage tests rerun with the corrected prompt — 24 November

Phil, 24 November: *"Rerun the baggage tests with the corrected prompt."*
All three tests (profiles A and B; *knows a lot* worked out per person;
Mixes C and D), one Sonnet run each, same customers and fixed experiences.

**What the prompt corrected:**
- Baggage is described as a field like the others, and it influences
  without being stated. People don't remark on service that met their
  expectations.
- The response length follows rule 52, using 2.5 words a second, which
  is my conversion **[C]**.
- It is a phone call, heard once, and the customer doesn't know whether
  this agent is the right one.
- The read adds a third question: heard once, can the agent follow it?

Results are in `logs/baggage-24-nov/rerun-test1-profiles.md`,
`rerun-test2-knows-a-lot.md` and `rerun-test3-mixes.md`. Lines that failed
my read are footnoted there.

or bad, before.
