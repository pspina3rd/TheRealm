On TheRealm, do not put working details in front of Phil — measurements,
tables, internal labels, lists of what was checked. **Record them** in a
file he can open if he wants, and in the reply tell him only when there
is a **problem**, offering **three solutions** for each. Game output he is
asked to look at must be readable as what it is: a conversation reads as
a conversation.

**Why:** On 2026-10-08 he said: "Record the details you seem ultra keen
on displaying to me, and focus on just letting me know when there is a
problem, and offering 3 solutions for it." It followed a day of reports
full of percentages and a "conversation" printed as a data form. It is
the same complaint as the info dumps he has objected to since September,
now with a concrete replacement.

**How to apply:** Write the detail to a log or notes file and name it in
one line. Lead the reply with the problem, in plain words, then three
distinct ways to solve it and which I'd pick. If there is no problem, say
what is done and stop. This does not waive
[[phil-raise-inventions-before-building]] — an invention that matters is
itself a problem to raise. Related: [[phil-write-strip-plus]],
[[phil-verify-by-reading-output]].

**Repeated 11 October 2026, in capitals: "how many times must I say DO NOT INFO DUMP".** It was said after a reply with three tables and five sections answering one question. A reply to Phil is a few lines: the answer, then the one thing needed from him. Tables, evidence lists and "what I verified" sections go in the notes file, with only a pointer in the reply. A long session does not earn a long report.
