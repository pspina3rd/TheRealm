Phil, 20 November: *"It's either correct or incorrect, not partially
correct or partially incorrect. Partial credit doesn't apply to your
outputs, its correct or it isn't. Stop gaslighting me and feeding me
partially completed work as completed work. Stop presenting correct
aspects surrounding incorrect aspects and calling your work completed."*

**What happened:** I reported step two of her words as having "worked",
listed what was good, and mentioned one wrong line near the end. He then
had to show me the line made no sense.

**How to apply:**
- If anything in the work is wrong, the work is not done. Say so first,
  plainly: "Not done. N things are wrong." Then the wrong things. Not the
  good things around them.
- Do not write "it worked", "it passes", "nearly", or a verdict, while a
  known fault remains. One wrong line means the run failed.
- Fix what can be fixed before reporting. What cannot be fixed without
  Phil is reported as what blocks completion, not as a footnote.
- Check every claim against the output before sending it; when unsure,
  say not done.
