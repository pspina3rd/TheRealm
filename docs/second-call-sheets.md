# Her sheet after the first call — three versions, for Phil to rule on

15 November. Mine [C]; nothing here is played or folded in until Phil
rules. The rules for what carries are the record's (`settled.md` §8, *It
grows*, *It misremembers*, *What gets written back*):

- What she knows changes when someone teaches her, and stays.
- How she feels about the co-op moves over several calls, so one call
  moves it a little.
- What she makes of an agent matters only on repeat contact.
- Personality barely moves.
- A moment is written only if it was felt. An ordinary competent call
  writes nothing.
- Only the residue carries: feeling and gist, not the words.
