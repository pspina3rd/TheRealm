When the decision log records different versions of a topic, use its latest explicit decision. Held choices remain held. The author has authorized editing the cinematic prose and trying the dawn sequence; this does not approve a new explanation of the voice or a replacement late-game plot.

## 2. The story at a glance

**Established opening.** Fern scouts for the Mossvale watch. His patrol ends at the stone circle, where the seven lights have always held steady. One night they flicker, then go out. Fern runs to Rowan. They return together and find the stones glowing again. Rowan believes Fern and decides he should learn magic to help protect Mossvale.

Rowan keeps the usual patrols, adds a dedicated watch at the stones and takes its first night himself. Fern receives an old rune stone Rowan found while digging the well. It does nothing in the opening and is not explained. Rowan recommends Kestrel at Windward Cliffs, but the player may choose another teacher first.

At dawn after Rowan's watch, Fern leaves quietly. Rowan alone sees him off. The illustrated opening ends as Fern passes the turning toward the stones and continues along the north road. The player controls the journey, regional approach tasks and lessons from there.

**Established larger structure.** The surface journey leads to the Moon Wyrm around the midpoint. Its defeat is a false victory: the continuing voice escapes the defeated creature. The second half has regions between Boss 2, Vesper's separate duel and Boss 3, followed by the return, Fennick's rite and Vesper's final form.

**Unfinished cause and effect.** Enchanted sleep is abandoned throughout the story. The exact replacement consequences of the stones' failure, the underground conflicts and the final recovery need a coherent no-sleep treatment. Do not preserve the old sleepers merely to keep those scenes working. The voice's nature is still a held choice.

## 3. The world and its places

Sevenstones is the surface overworld. The stone circle, or sanctuary, is one specific site within it. Only Mossvale connects directly to it; other regions reach it through Mossvale. Do not turn Sevenstones into the name of the starting settlement.

The existing map and teacher homes remain fixed. Fern is from Mossvale; Lance from Cairnwick in Redrock, Ares from Merehaven in Glasswater, and Vex from Bramblecross in Thornwood. The same familiar opening cast occurs once in the selected hometown, not four duplicated casts. Fern's specific family history is not automatically assigned to every hero.

## 4. Magic and the crisis

There are seven schools, each with four trainable ranks. Heroes begin with zero spells. Affinity does not grant a spell. The player chooses teachers and development order; Kestrel is guidance for Fern, not a mandatory Wind unlock. Earlier decisions prescribing Quake at Rowan's well or Dorn as the forced first teacher are superseded.

The six base schools are available before the Wyrm. Arcane and custom Weaving remain post-Wyrm, with Hareguy's awakened gift as the planned catalyst. Hareguy's appearance before the Wyrm must not depend on delivery of the optional letter. The exact meaning of having learned “the magics” must be clarified before adding any new required-school gate; do not silently require every optional lesson.

| School    | Board effect                                                                                                  |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fire      | Burns rows; ranks cover one through four rows.                                                                |
| Ice       | Fills gaps with colorless material. It earns no mana.                                                         |
| Gravity   | Drops unsupported material; increasing reach through Gravity, Mudslide, Earthquake and Avalanche.             |
| Wind      | Moves eligible material sideways where room exists, including movable obstructions.                           |
| Lightning | Damages enemy obstructions and opens space when they break.                                                   |
| Nature    | Converts obstructions into harmless blank material, possibly over repeated casts. The material earns no mana. |
| Arcane    | Seventh, opponent-targeted school. Primary Shadow and advanced techniques are distinct from custom Weaving.   |

Custom Weaving combines spells with player-chosen order and strength, determining cost. It does not replace the Arcane school. Follow [the casting contract](BOARD_MAGIC_PLAN.md) for detailed behavior.

The standing stones are different pack-art boulders: grey when unlit, dark with colored veins and flecks when lit. They form an old, slightly uneven ring. Violet goes out first in the opening; that order has no special meaning there.

The old rune stone is a separate hand-held object, not one of those seven stones. No magical effect is demonstrated in the opening. The isolated web playtest records possession without awarding the old equippable Focus Stone, which would change piece odds. That is a prototype boundary, not a settled later inventory design.

No enchanted-sleep epidemic, lamplighting order or Lamplighter secret history belongs in the revised story. The well contributed to the wider problem, but the opening does not explain how.

## 5. The people

### Fern

He/him. Ranger and Mossvale scout. He is restless, wants excitement and is still grieving his father. Rowan has helped raise him for ten years. Learning magic excites him; leaving familiar ground unsettles him. These feelings can coexist without an explicit speech about each one.

Show his competence in observation and movement. Do not make him an experienced world traveler, a hidden chosen one or merely a messenger who waits for every decision.

### Rowan and Bracken

Rowan is the current watch captain. Bracken, Fern's father, was his childhood best friend and the previous captain. Rowan is nearly a second father to Fern. Bad knees are not his reason for staying home; his duty to Mossvale and guilt matter instead.

Ten years ago, a Redrock collapse trapped one of Dorn's crews. Bracken took rescuers in. Rowan called a retreat; Bracken kept getting others out. Rowan pulled the last miner clear as Bracken pushed him from inside, turned to help Bracken, and the rocks fell. Why Rowan blames himself should emerge when he talks about it. Do not reduce this to his “inaction.”

Dorn was there. The account belongs later, when Fern reaches him and explains his journey, not in opening exposition.

### Hareguy and Rowan's wife

Hareguy is Rowan's son, alive and awake in Brightwater. He and Fern already know each other. He wants to be a hair stylist; Rowan has not taken that ambition seriously. His name is pronounced Hair-gway; Mossvale's Hair Guy version should be shown through interaction rather than a pronunciation lecture.

His mother, Rowan's wife, was an Elf. She believed his latent abilities would awaken after a sign involving the stones. Elves are rare; most have left, and remaining Elves may be hidden. Elvish language is not used in the story except through names.

The rift with Rowan is not a checklist for one dialogue scene. Let Hareguy voice the parts that matter to him. His magical usefulness must not replace his own ambition and emotional life.

### The letter

An optional task after the played first-lesson trip and return home. Fern must not read it. It contains Rowan's apology, admission that his wife was right and request that Hareguy return. The player sees Hareguy read and react, not the letter's words. Delivery changes his greeting, not whether he can enter the main story.

### Other familiar people and mentors

Bean has her garden and livelihood; Ordor has his trade; Pip's tower is a cooperative task, not hostile obstruction placement. Preserve their ordinary concerns. The revised morning and departure have no neighbor cameos. Kestrel's dread that same night remains unexplained. She does not identify or activate the rune stone when Fern shows it to her.

### Vesper, Fennick and the voice

Vesper uses they/them; Fennick uses he/him. Vesper's duel and evolved final form are separate required encounters. The proposed motive of wanting to be heard, Fennick's twelve children and his specific failure remain held. Do not import those proposals as approved history.

The voice's identity and limits are unresolved. The earlier Lamplighter order and shared order histories for Irdrath, the Lishal, Moonfall and the ward were rejected. “Ves, en.” is removed. Fennick's replacement English wording is still subject to final confirmation.

## 7. The monomyth

The opening provides ordinary life through Fern's patrol, a call through the impossible stone failure, guidance through Rowan and a threshold at the first step beyond the familiar route. Fern's excitement does not require adding an artificial refusal speech. Fear, attachment and Rowan's reasons for staying can supply tension through action.

| Rough narrative position | Major beat                                                     |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Around 50%               | Moon Wyrm: Ordeal and false victory; continuing voice escapes. |
| Around 62%               | Boss 2; team-source identity Irdrath.                          |
| Around 72%               | Separate Vesper duel.                                          |

These are structural targets, not measured playtime. Keep real regions and player activity between confrontations. The monomyth should clarify choices and consequences, not become exposition spoken by the characters.

## 10. Language and presentation

Use consistent third-person narration and past tense where narration is needed. Label dialogue speakers. Let the picture carry entrances, running, sitting, restored light and exchanged objects. Keep words for character speech, essential context and interiority worth retaining. Do not remove all literary narration from the in-world book.

Avoid recounting a scene the player just watched unless the report introduces a decision or new information. Rowan's practical care should emerge through listening and acting, not an explanation of how well he reads Fern's expressions. Keep his affection without making every line a lesson. Let Fern's excitement and apprehension coexist.

Book navigation must preserve reading pace, safe skip and a clear return to play. Animation duration is not a reading deadline. Desktop visual review and actual iPhone review are separate validations.
