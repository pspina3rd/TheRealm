# How each of the ten runs ended, from each seat — 15 November

A working file of mine [C], not for the record. Phil, 14 November: *"The
view of a problem is based on the perspective of the viewer."* I had been
reading every run from one seat. This reads each ending from three: hers,
the agent's, and TLC's (what the record holds afterwards, and what TLC
gets next). Each line is taken from what was said in that run, not from the
type of ending.

Runs 1–5 are `docs/playthroughs-14-nov-replay.md`; runs 6–10 are
`docs/playthroughs-14-nov-runs6-10.md`.

## Run 1 — "It looks like you changed the bag"

- **Hers:** she had told this agent she was worried sick, and was then
  told she shut Pell out herself. When she objected, the agent pointed at
  the record. The bag is open. She stopped talking to the agent.
- **The agent's:** found it and fixed it on play eight. She took it
  badly, and they don't know why.
- **TLC's:** the setting is reversed, and this is the one run where the
  true cause was said aloud. She can't Assign, so the next item she wants
  to hand over brings her back.
- **Where it sprouts:** the agent, and the record that backed the agent.

## Run 2 — "Your party bag was changed"

- **Hers:** she poured out her fear for Pell and was answered with "I
  completely understand. So Pell needs the draught." She was asked for an
  order number she never had. Then it was fixed, and it had been "something
  on your side" all along.
- **The agent's:** fixed on the last play. They read out what the profile
  said. They don't know she filled in who did it.
- **TLC's:** reversed. She now believes in a TLC fault that never
  happened. She can't Assign, so she will hunt through her settings again
  the next time.
- **Where it sprouts:** the company for the cause; the agent for not
  listening.

## Run 3 — "So he can just take it?"

- **Hers:** fixed, she thinks. Nobody said why it happened. Her last
  question went unanswered.
- **The agent's:** began by looking for a faulty bag, guessed the wrong
  Sella Brandt, and was put right by checking with her. Fixed on the last
  play.
- **TLC's:** reversed. No cause given, no Assign.
- **Where it sprouts:** nowhere much. A thin ending, not a bad one.

## Run 4 — "Is that good?"

- **Hers:** she was told "It was on Admin Only. I've taken it off," and
  does not know that means it is fixed. She still believes nothing changed
  on her side.
- **The agent's:** fixed, job done.
- **TLC's:** reversed. She may well ring again tonight, with the bag
  already working.
- **Where it sprouts:** the service: they talk in their own words.

## Run 5 — "Assign. That's what I couldn't find."

- **Hers:** called Stella, asked whether Pell was doing it right, asked
  what her own account was set to. Then fixed, and given the word she had
  been hunting for all night. She ended warm.
- **The agent's:** clumsy in the middle, strong at the end.
- **TLC's:** reversed, and she can Assign. The only run so far where this
  problem will not bring her back.
- **Where it sprouts:** nothing negative that she voiced. The ending
  covered the middle.

## Run 6 — "Fine."

- **Hers:** told the bag was "set up as it should be", then told it had
  been changed and was changed back. She shut down, and set her own next
  step.
- **The agent's:** fixed on play seven. Asked a closing question and got
  an answer with nothing in it.
- **TLC's:** reversed. No Assign.
- **Where it sprouts:** the agent, a little: they said one thing and then
  the opposite.

## Run 7 — "I never asked for it."

- **Hers:** fixed, with next steps. She is left with an open question: how
  did it get set to that?
- **The agent's:** the cleanest call of the ten. Privately they concluded
  she changed it on purpose, which is wrong, and did not say so.
- **TLC's:** reversed, next steps given. No Assign. If the agent's private
  conclusion goes in the case notes, the record is wrong.
- **Where it sprouts:** nothing yet. The open question is a seed with
  nowhere to land so far.

## Run 8 — "So you can't tell me."

- **Hers:** told to wait an hour after a night of trying. Then fixed. She
  worked out for herself that she may have pressed something, asked how to
  give one thing to one person, and got a goodbye.
- **The agent's:** one wrong fix, then the right one on the last play.
  They heard her question and had no play left for it.
- **TLC's:** reversed. She has asked for Assign in so many words and not
  been told, so a repeat call is close to certain.
- **Where it sprouts:** the service: they can fix it and can't tell you
  how to do it.

## Run 9 — "Something on your side shut my man out"

- **Hers:** listened to, understood, given Assign. And TLC locked Pell
  out the night before a contract.
- **The agent's:** a good call.
- **TLC's:** reversed, and she can Assign. She believes in a TLC fault
  that never happened, and the agent's own word put it there.
- **Where it sprouts:** the company only. The agent is thanked.

## Run 10 — "But he'll have it."

- **Hers:** fixed on the last play. Nobody said why. She is tired and
  relieved.
- **The agent's:** misread her twice, recovered, fixed.
- **TLC's:** reversed. No cause given, no Assign.
- **Where it sprouts:** nowhere much.

## What reading it this way showed me

1. **The three seats disagree most on the good calls.** Runs 7 and 9 are
   the agent's best, and each leaves something wrong behind that the agent
   cannot see: a wrong private conclusion in run 7, a false belief about
   TLC in run 9.
2. **From TLC's seat, eight of the ten calls are not finished.** The bag
   is open, but she still cannot Assign, so the same need will bring her
   back. Only runs 5 and 9 close it.
3. **The ten runs are one call played ten times, not ten calls in a
   row.**[^1] She is held the same in each so the sheet can be tested. In
   the game her sheet would grow from call to call, like a character in a
   long campaign, so each ending above is a different place her second
   call would start from.
4. **The agent never learns what it planted.** In no run does the agent's
   seat show the mark left on hers. That is `settled.md` §4: the game
   never confirms it.

[^1]: I first wrote here that fixing the symptom without giving her the
    word might be why "she does not know she has called repeatedly"
    (Phil's list, 16 October). Wrong. Phil, 15 November: that line was
    *"keeping the customer variable the same across multiple runs"*. He did
    not want the sheet carried across the runs, because it *"would be
    growing like a rpg character would from interaction to interaction,
    it's like a multi-year long campaign. If the NPCs didn't grow and
    evolve the story would be boring."*
