# Dialogue draft — Bell Hollows to the ascent

**Working source draft; new interpretations remain proposed.** Scene dialogue
has a playable adaptation in [campaignStory.ts](../../src/data/campaignStory.ts);
not every stage direction or tentative mechanic is implemented there. These scenes
follow [SCENES_DRAFT.md](../SCENES_DRAFT.md), using the team's Irdrath,
seven-member Lishal, and Malificat names from the
[encounter-design appendix](https://github.com/pspina3rd/TheRealm/blob/d14b3c4f6bb173986e05af4ce89e9895c437a34b/docs/ENCOUNTER_DESIGN.md).
That appendix's second-half mechanics remain tentative. Existing first-half
and mentor dialogue is not rewritten. Speaker numbers distinguish the
Lishal's seven singers; they are not new personal names or biographies.
Ketter's bellwork and Odile's refuge are proposed roles for the team's named
second-half residents, not established history.

## 09 · Ketter cannot leave the bell

_A bell strikes twice. Ketter puts down a spoon and answers on a smaller bell.
A packed tool roll rests beside a bowl with a skin across its surface._

**Hero:** You made it back. How is your lift?

**Ketter:** Working, thanks to you. It's the other one now.

_Three quick strikes. Ketter listens, then answers once._

**Ketter:** Lift's stopped between landings. Again.

**Hero:** Who's on it?

**Ketter:** Two from the east works. They've been waiting for supper longer
than I have. Hold this cord. I'll show you where it's caught.

_The hero helps free the return cord and repair the local signal circuit.
A worker takes the bell while Ketter tests the signal from the landing._

**Ketter:** Two for hold. One for—

**Worker:** I know. You've told me every shift this week.

_The worker answers a call correctly. Ketter's hand stops short of the cord._

**Ketter:** Right. You have it.

**Hero:** You can show me the relay now?

_Ketter opens a drawer. Beneath the plans lies a bundle of letters._

**Ketter:** These came from Fennick. Take them too. They wanted measurements
we couldn't get while the lower bells were running.

_One letter shows the surface stations of the ward. The line marked
"lower return" ends at a blank space. Ketter lays it beside the relay plan._

**Hero:** And now?

**Ketter:** Now someone's got to go down and look.

_Ketter takes the tool roll, then lifts the bowl._

**Ketter:** Give me a moment. I'm finishing this outside.

## 10 · The Lishal receives a refusal

_Seven singers stand around the foundry's great sounding frame. The second
carries a steaming pot to the workers. The first binds a burned hand; the
third rolls out bedding. The fifth checks the shutter chain while the
seventh tends the frame's seven crystal sockets. The fourth and sixth keep
the machinery turning._

**Lishal, first singer:** Sit here. Your hand will swell if you keep using it.

**Worker:** I've a cart outside. I'll eat on the way.

**Lishal, fourth singer:** Your meal's ready. Leave the cart a while.

**Ketter:** That our repair box? The one under the bench?

_The fourth singer slides it across. Ketter checks the tools._

**Worker:** Thank you for the bandage. I need my coat.

**Lishal, third singer:** There's a bed behind the curtain.

**Worker:** You told us about the sleep. I heard you. I don't want it.

_The first singer keeps hold of the end of the bandage._

**Lishal, sixth singer:** They understand. Let them take the coat.

_The worker reaches for it. The fourth singer turns a catch beside the door.
The third pulls the bedding across the passage. The second sets down the
pot and blocks the other exit. The sixth looks at the catch, then returns
to their place at the frame._

**Lishal, together:** Close the outer doors. Bring the beds through.

**Hero:** Ketter. The loading door.

_Ketter wedges it with the repair box. The hero helps the workers through.
The fifth singer lowers a shutter; it strikes one worker's leg. The seventh
reaches toward the injured worker, then draws back to keep their place at
the frame. Ketter goes back for the worker._

**Worker:** Your box—

**Ketter:** Leave it. Arm round my shoulder. Can you manage that?

_The sixth singer sees the open loading door and adds their voice to the
others. The sounding frame begins to turn faster._

## 11 · Vesper makes the offer

_From the inspection gallery, the hero can see Vesper on the far platform.
The moving frame crosses the gap between them. Its passage leaves no room
to follow. The footbridge release is beside the hero's hand. A diagram on
the inspection rail completes the missing lower return in Fennick's letter;
the hero copies its connections before approaching the release._

**Vesper:** Listen to it. Seven voices, and they can barely carry a whisper.

**Voice:** You need not wait for them.

**Vesper:** I have waited quite enough.

_Light spreads through the stone beneath Vesper's feet._

**Voice:** Take my voice with you. The stones will answer you wherever you go.

**Hero:** Vesper. I've got the bridge.

_The hero releases it. The footbridge lowers along the outside of the
moving frame, opening a retreat into the inspection gallery. Before the
hero can cross, a warning bell sounds below. The loading shutter begins
to fall across the workers' exit. The hero catches its brake and holds it._

**Vesper:** You found a way across.

**Hero:** Come over.

_Vesper looks at the bridge. They take one step toward it, close enough
for the hero to see the cut left by their duel._

**Vesper:** And let you shut all this away?

**Hero:** You read the same names I did. The other vessels.

**Vesper:** I read every one.

_The sounding frame passes between them. Vesper's fingers close around
the gallery rail. When it passes again, they have let go._

**Hero:** Vesper?

**Vesper:** No. I'm staying.

_They turn toward the light._

**Vesper:** Let me hear it. All of it.

_The light climbs their arms. Vesper draws breath; the nearby bells sound
with it. They stagger once, then straighten. Their next words reach the
hero from the stone overhead as well as the far platform._

**Vesper:** I can hear the surface.

_They leave by the service ascent marked on Ketter's plan. The hero keeps
hold of the shutter brake while the last workers pass through the loading
door. Ketter signals when they are clear. The hero can release it at last._

## 12 · The work continues without permission

_The hero returns to the relay controls. Ketter has the last workers clear.
Their relief operator answers through the local bell._

**Ketter:** Lift calls are still coming through. Try the other handle.

_The hero disconnects the branch feeding the great sounding frame. The
small bell answers. The great frame slows, its seven colours fading._

**Lishal, fourth singer:** Put it back.

**Hero:** The lifts are working. Your people are out.

**Lishal, first singer:** They won't stay out. They'll tire. They'll be hurt.

**Ketter:** They'll send for help. Like they did today.

_The first singer takes up the loose drive handle. The sixth catches it
and, for a moment, holds it still._

**Lishal, sixth singer:** The rooms are empty.

**Lishal, fourth singer:** There are others above.

_The sixth singer fits the handle into the manual drive. All seven resume
their places, each before a different colour of crystal._

**Lishal, together:** Begin again.

_Boss 3: the seven singers defend the manual drive. On victory, their
connection to the sounding frame breaks. The local lift bell remains lit.
No new ritual document appears as a boss reward: the hero already carries
the copied procedure assembled from the archive, letters, and inspection._

**Ketter:** There. Hear that? Someone's reached the upper landing.

_The hero checks the service-ascent plan. Vesper has a head start._

## 13 · People use the road

_At the upper landing, a fallen beam blocks the lift. Odile waits with the
refuge's residents. Two bearers hold a sleeper on a blanket between them._

**Odile:** Set her down. Both of you. The floor's dry here.

**Hero:** Vesper came this way?

**Odile:** Straight through. Didn't stop.

_The hero looks toward the stairs. Ketter sets a jack under the beam._

**Ketter:** I need someone on the wheel. I'll have to get underneath.

**Odile:** I can turn it.

**Ketter:** You need to keep the landing clear.

_The hero takes the wheel. Odile moves the waiting group back._

**Hero:** Tell me when.

**Ketter:** Slowly. That's it. Hold there.

_The beam rises. Ketter moves beneath it and frees the lift cable. The relief
operator's bell answers from below. Ketter begins to give an instruction,
then hears the lift start correctly._

**Ketter:** Good. They've got it.

_Odile directs the first bearers onto the lift. A courier descends the
stairs, shielding a lamp from the draught._

**Courier:** Is the road open? Fennick sent me. The violet stone's going dark.

**Hero:** We're coming.

**Odile:** Go on. We can move them now.

_She takes a bearer's place while they rest. Ketter remains at the landing
to guide the next lift. The hero climbs toward Sevenstones with the plan
and copied procedure. Behind them, the first group reaches the dry floor._
