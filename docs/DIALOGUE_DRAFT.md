Hero lines use a common voice that works for any class. Familiar mentor
lines are used only for relationships the player actually earned; missing
relationships have a practical fallback. These are scene exchanges, not a
replacement set of every NPC's rotating greetings or encounter barks.

Odile's waterkeeping and Ketter's bellwork are proposed roles for existing
team cast names. Fennick's connection to Vesper, the rite's exact operation,
and Vesper surviving remain working story choices. No translation is assigned
to the source's “Ves, en.” The newly written dialogue leaves the team's
first-half text and existing mentor Road Back lines unchanged.
