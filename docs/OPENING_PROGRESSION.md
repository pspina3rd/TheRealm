# Playable opening and teacher access

The opening is a village problem the player can investigate. It is not a list
of game systems. Mossvale has separate households, the well, herb beds, tower
yard and stall. The sleeping houses can be examined directly. One short lead
points to the next immediate action; optional tasks and regional exploration
remain available.

The player chooses schools by whom they visit and help. **Rowan still teaches
Quake through the well encounter.** Orrin's roof task still teaches Thunder.
Neither reward is removed or replaced by a forced mentor order.

## Earned story beats

Investigating a sleeping household and clearing the well earns Rowan's call
and Focus Stone scene in Mossvale. Simply crossing the town boundary gives
neither. The first vision follows the earned call on departure. Vesper's early
appearances wait until that call and a Brightwater visit, including alternate
routes through Quarry or Peaks. The Ranger's reluctance describes concern for
the sleepers rather than claiming the player has never traveled.

All regional school routes can be explored before the call. The Moon Caves need
both the Cave Pass and the earned call; a player who obtains the pass first is
directed back to Rowan. This prevents defeating the Wyrm before the opening's
reason to leave has happened. No spell count, chosen school or character level
is required at that boundary.

These are real collision boundaries with marked entrances. Keyboard movement
and tap paths obey the same rules. Completing the task opens the path and shows
the result. Teachers stay at home until their first lesson is won, then resume
their established routes. The existing post-Wyrm Arcane unlock remains.

Previously completed tasks and mentor meetings retain access. Old saves inside
an added boundary can leave without receiving free inward access. Save version
15 preserves an established pre-update journey without fabricating a
Brightwater visit, teaching spells or granting another Focus Stone.

## Board instruction and feedback

Offers show the immediate task and reward. Detailed consequences remain under
**Rewards & risks**, with the immediate failure risk still visible. Board
headers use the actual task; internal cooperation/enemy classifications are not
player-facing explanations.

Bean's green crystals take root as they land in green beds. Paths and other
colors earn no sprouts. Her demonstration visibly earns four sprouts without
requiring a line clear. Previously planted seeds never score twice when a row
later clears; mana still comes from line clears. The task finishes at its goal;
the optional herb bonus rewards finishing within twelve drops.
