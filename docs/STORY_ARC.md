# Mana Crystals — full-story structure and writer brief

This records Philip Spina's approved story structure. It governs the full
campaign; [STORY.md](STORY.md) contains the existing first-half dialogue.
The expanded second-half scenes and dialogue still need to be written and
implemented. The monomyth is the underlying narrative framework.

A proposed [second-half outline](STORY_OUTLINE_DRAFT.md) is available for
review. Its new names, lore, and mechanisms are draft proposals;
they are not approved canon or implemented game content.

## Preserve the first half

Keep Mossvale, Brightwater, the seven mentor regions, and the Moon Caves,
with the Moon Wyrm at the end of this existing material. Preserve the current
first-half scenes and dialogue, subject to the two continuity fixes below.

The Moon Wyrm marks approximately the midpoint of the full game. Its defeat
is the Ordeal and a false victory: the beast falls, but the Master's voice
escapes it. It is not the final victory of the full campaign.

## Expand the second half

Build an underground second half of roughly the same length as the first.
Place substantial regions, exploration, and connective story between the
major encounters. These percentages are rough narrative pacing targets,
not implemented progress calculations or time limits.

| Approximate progress | Major beat                                                               |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 50%                  | Moon Wyrm: midpoint Ordeal and false victory; the Master's voice escapes |
| 62%                  | Boss 2                                                                   |
| 72%                  | Vesper duel                                                              |
| 82%                  | Boss 3                                                                   |
| 85–95%               | Return to Sevenstones and Fennick's rite                                 |
| End                  | Vesper's final form                                                      |

Bosses 2 and 3 remain unnamed in this brief. The Vesper duel and final form
are separate major encounters. Do not collapse them into the Wyrm encounter
or move the duel after Boss 3.

The current build ends at the Wyrm and leaves its sequel ward sealed.
That is an implementation boundary, not the intended end of the full story.

## Two continuity fixes

1. The opening must say only some households in Mossvale will not wake.
   Rowan, Bea, and Odo are awake in their existing scenes.
2. Pip's mother falling asleep must fit the lines where Pip quotes her.
   If the writer cannot make those scenes consistent, choose a different
   motivation for the hero to leave. Do not silently contradict Pip's dialogue.

The [team's updated script](https://github.com/pspina3rd/TheRealm/blob/fe5c3e49473297c008b2167f8476c043db735d0f/docs/STORY.md)
already limits the sleeping households and supplies Rowan's sleeping son as
the Refusal's personal stake. It uses Bean and Ordor for Bea and Odo. Consult
that script alongside GetPutTaco's older first-half text when developing
characters. Its Wyrm survives and sleeps, some sleepers stir, and the voice
speaks again at Sevenstones. Preserve those details in the continuation.

## Next writing deliverable

Develop the characters before writing dialogue. Consult the existing script,
then establish who each person is, what they want, what they need, what they
fear, how they feel in the scene, and how those pressures shape their choices
and speech. The working profiles are in [CHARACTERS_DRAFT.md](CHARACTERS_DRAFT.md).
Existing facts and proposed characterization must stay distinguishable.

Draft the expanded outline before adding production encounters:

- Identify the underground regions and the events connecting each major beat.
- Establish Bosses 2 and 3, their motivations, and their role in the central conflict.
- Write the Wyrm aftermath so the false victory and escaping voice are clear.
- Develop Vesper's later duel and final transformation as distinct story beats.
- Connect the return to Sevenstones and Fennick's rite to the ending.
- Resolve both continuity fixes while preserving the existing first-half material.

Review that outline against this brief and the existing script, then write
the scenes and dialogue. New lore, boss identities, region names, and the
rite's details should be presented as proposals until approved.

## Development and validation after writing

Implement the approved regions, encounters, story flags, and save migrations
in playable increments, keeping the team branches aligned. Extend dialogue
fidelity and story-transition tests as new scenes are added.

Existing Wyrm victory metrics measure the implemented first-half endpoint.
Full-campaign completion must eventually mean victory over Vesper's final
form. Keep current-build, first-half, and full-campaign results labelled
explicitly while the remaining content is under development.

A human keyboard and touch playthrough remains necessary to assess narrative
clarity, spacing, motivation, and the experience of the false victory.
