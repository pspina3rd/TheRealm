# The opening test — 24 November

Phil, 24 November: *"Record that, then run the opening test."* Hob, Wren
and Ilse (`docs/customers.md`) were each handed each of TLC's eight
categories (`docs/card-rules.md`, 44–47) and nothing else; what they do in
their lives turned it into their own experience, which they explained in
reply to the universal greeting. One Sonnet subagent wrote the 24; I read
every one by Phil's two questions and his reading method. The openings are
in `logs/openings-24-nov/openings.md`.

Sella was left out: her baggage on her sheet is about Pell's contract, so
her sheet is still tied to the bag call.

Three openings failed my read and were rewritten: Hob's name *"Two b's in
Hob"* (his name has one); Wren saying both *"I only had a quick look"* and
*"I did check it twice"*; Ilse's *"Once should have been enough"*, which
left the listener to work out what she meant.

## Overlaps within a category

The same category, in three lives, produced openings that share a core:

- **Tracking** — all three: *it should be here by now; where is it?*
  Hob's iron, Wren's ingredients, Ilse's stock.
- **Access** — all three: *someone can't see what I can see on your side.*
  One of the company's men, a new journeyman, Ilse's husband.
- **Order** — Hob and Ilse: *I'm trying to buy something and it won't go
  through / keeps being rejected.* Wren: *I put an order through and can't
  tell if it went.*
- **Repair** — Hob and Ilse: *it came back, and the work isn't right.*
- **Account** — Hob and Wren: *the bills are going to the wrong (or an
  old) address.*
- **Shipment** — *what came isn't right*: the wrong kind (Hob), maybe the
  wrong thing (Wren), part missing (Ilse).

## Overlaps between categories

Openings that could be filed under more than one category, which is where
an agent's choice of category differs and a later search can miss the case
(rule 45):

- **Wren, User** (*messages from you keep not reaching me; maybe your
  details for me are wrong, or it's our side*) — User, Account, or
  Navigation.
- **Hob, Account** (*the company's bills go to the wrong address*) and
  **Ilse, User** (*the number you have for me is out of date*) — Account
  and User, depending on whose detail it is.
- **Ilse, Access** (*how do I let my husband see our records?*) — Access
  or Account.
- **Hob and Ilse, Navigation** (*which of your services do I use to send
  this?*) — Navigation, Order or Shipment.
- **Wren, Shipment** (*one jar might be the wrong thing*) — Shipment or
  Order, if she ordered it wrong.
- **Hob and Ilse, Repair** (*it came back badly mended*) — Repair, or
  Shipment if it is the wrong item back.

## Out of their depth

Where the category did not fit their usual life, it showed as rule 47
says: Hob, ringing about the company's bills, says *"Changing that isn't my
job, but the man who does it is away"*; Wren, about the guild's address,
*"I'm not even sure it's mine to change"*; Wren, about a repair she didn't
send, *"This might be the wrong place to ask"* (marked as barely fitting).
