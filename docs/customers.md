# Customers for finding the patterns in requests — from 22 November

Phil, 22 November: contemplate the types of request that come in. The
universal greeting prompts each customer's own account of what led them to
call; their sheet makes it unique; finding what several customers share
will show where descriptions overlap between categories of request
(`docs/card-rules.md`, 37). Sheets proposed one at a time (*"Propose
sheets one at a time"*). Everything on each sheet is mine **[C]** unless
marked; each is accepted by Phil before the next is proposed.

**Characters are independent of requests (Phil, 23 November).** *"What good
does it do to design a character if you are requiring a customer service
reason to create them? The customer service reason is the variable that
they react to when presented it, which could be any in the game!"* So each
customer below is the character alone: who they are and where they stand
(the account and individual posts of the fence), their sheet, how they
share, their customer-service baggage, how they talk, and their notebook.
The requests are a separate list at the end; any customer can be handed
any of them, and what they know about it comes from the request plus who
they are. I first wrote each customer around one request; those requests
have been moved out, and nothing about the characters depended on them.

## Customer 1 — Hob Tallis · accepted 22 November (*"Looks good."*)

- **The individual:** a smith who keeps the blades for a small mercenary
  company; ringing as the company's contact, not for himself.
- **The account:** the company's, not his (the account post and the
  individual post are different people).
- **Customer-service baggage:** he has been told before that he was
  mistaken about something he was not, and expects it again.
- **Sheet:** repetition high (wants things said back) · empathy low ·
  pattern high · non-positive middle · casting wide.
- **How he shares, where he usually sits:** volume low · signal high ·
  certainty high · accuracy very high on anything to do with metal,
  ordinary otherwise · openness low.
- **How he talks:** short, exact about his trade, no small talk.

### Hob's notebook

Phil, 22 November: the personal part of a sheet is the character's
notebook, *"the piece of the puzzle that says here is who he is, what he
might inject into a conversation, who he might mention, what he might
gossip about, what he might care about, etc."* Like a separate notebook per
character loaded when they call in, done through programming, not AI. A
first set of six personal areas was rejected as forced, oddly specific and
poorly worded; withdrawn.

Sections [C]: people · what he cares about · what he'd gossip or grumble
about · opinions · pastimes (*small toys for children* **[P]**) · things
that have happened to him · rivals.

**What he says from it passes through memory by type** (`settled.md` §8,
*It misremembers*): the gist holds; dates, names and exact words slip. And
calls write to it.

**1. People** · accepted 22 November (*"Yeah that makes sense."*)
- *"My wife's Ada. She sells eggs at the market on market days."*
- *"We've two boys. Tam's eight and wants to be a smith. Jory's five and
  wants to join the company when he's older. I'd rather he didn't."*[^p1]
- *"Ada's mother moved in with us after the winter. It's been a long
  spring."*
- *"Bren and I grew up on the same street. He drives a cart now, and we
  have a drink at the Anvil most evenings. He always has news from the
  road."*
- *"Our dog's called Nail. He sleeps by the forge."*[^p1]

[^p1]: Reworded 22 November after Phil: *"Watch your phrasing, some of it is
    questionable."* Was *"which I'm not having"*, a regional turn of
    phrase, and *"There's a dog, Nail. He sleeps by the forge because it's
    warm"*, which doesn't say whose dog it is and explains the obvious. I
    first changed *"It's been a long spring"* instead; Phil: *"wrong on
    number 2. Try the dog."* Put back.

**2. What he cares about** · accepted 22 November (*"Yes"*)
- *"Ada and the boys."*
- *"Work done right. Nothing leaves my forge that isn't."*
- *"Paying what I owe when I owe it. I expect the same."*
- *"My tools. Most of them were my master's."*
- *"Straight answers."*

**3. What he'd gossip or grumble about** · accepted 22 November (*"Yes"*)
- *"Iron costs more every month."*
- *"The company brings me every blade the night before they march."*
- *"Ada's mother has something to say about everything I do."*
- *"Bren's got a story for everything, and it gets bigger every time he

Withdrawn on the way: *"The company's men treat their blades badly, then
wonder why they need work"* (no one believes that), *"…then wonder"* and
*"Bren says a lot of things"* (a sneer on top of second-hand gossip), *"Bren
tells me the north road's closed again"* (nothing to him, and nothing to an
agent who doesn't know the town), *"The company always wants its blades back
the same day"* (sometimes that is simply needed).

**4. Opinions** · accepted 22 November (*"Yes"*)
- *"I don't like sending work out. Sometimes there's no choice."*
- *"A boy should learn a trade before he picks up a sword."*
- *"Most trouble comes from someone rushing."*

Withdrawn: *"POOF's fine for most things. I'd still rather take a blade to
the repairer myself"* — he is the one who repairs the company's blades.
Why he sent this sword out is left open and can differ from call to call
(Phil: the opinion applies to all the reasons, and to many other scenarios
for him).

**5. Pastimes** · 22 November
- *"I like to make small trinkets, children really seem to like them."*
  **[P]** (Phil's wording)
- *"I spend my rest days with the boys."*

Withdrawn: *"Little horses, mostly"* (no reason to say what kind), *"I take
the boys down to the river"* (too specific), *"for the children on our
street"* (limits him to his street).

**6. Things that have happened to him** · accepted 23 November (*"Yes"*)
- *"I was apprenticed at twelve."*
- *"My master left me the forge when she died."*
- *"I broke my wrist two winters ago. Couldn't work for a month."*

**7. Rivals and people he doesn't get on with** · 23 November
- *"There's a smith who settled nearby. He charges less and some say you
  get what you pay for…"* **[P]** (Phil's wording, in place of mine: *"a
  smith across town who charges half what I do. You get what you pay
  for."*)
- *"The company's quartermaster argues over every bill."*

**Hob's notebook is complete.**

## Customer 2 — Wren Ashby · call sheet taken as standing 23 November

(Proposed 22 November; Phil went on to the notebook without changing it:
*"Use the template we established for Hob."*)

- **The individual:** a journeyman at the alchemists' guild who places the
  ingredient orders for the guild workshop.
- **The account:** the guild's; she is one of many contacts on it.
- **Customer-service baggage:** last time she rang, she was passed between
  three people before anyone helped.
- **Sheet:** repetition low · empathy high · pattern middle · non-positive
  low · no strong lean.
- **How she shares, where she usually sits:** volume high · signal middle ·
  certainty low · accuracy middle · openness high.
- **How she talks:** quick, apologetic, explains more than she needs to,
  thinks out loud.

### Wren's notebook

Same sections as Hob's, written as she would think about them.

**1. People** · accepted 23 November (*"Yes"*)
- *"My mum's a seamstress. She wanted me to go into the trade with her, but
  I think she's proud of me now. I think."*
- *"My brother's in the city watch. I worry about him, though he says I
  shouldn't."*
- *"I share rooms with Pip, another journeyman. She's the tidy one, if I'm
  honest."*
- *"I've been seeing Tom, from the bakery. It's still new, so I don't say
  much about it. Well, I try not to."*

**2. What she cares about** · accepted 23 November
- *"Mum, my brother, Pip. Tom too, I suppose."*
- *"Getting things right. I check everything twice, then once more to be
  sure."*
- *"Not letting the workshop down."*
- *"People being kind to each other. It doesn't cost anything."*

**3. What she'd gossip or grumble about** · 23 November
- *"Mum keeps asking when she's going to meet Tom."*
- *"My brother never comes to visit."* (her openness lets personal things
  slip; Phil: *"I don't dislike your justification"*)
- *"The workshop's always cold first thing."* (she works there every
  morning; not the guild hall, where she would not be often enough to know)

Withdrawn: the price grumble (prices don't touch her; the guild pays), the
retirement gossip (an odd thing to tell a stranger), and the workshop gossip
she shared and then withheld.

**4. Opinions** · accepted 23 November
- *"If you're not sure, ask. I'd rather look silly than get it wrong."*
- *"Most people are doing their best."*
- *"A workshop runs on people helping each other."*

**5. Pastimes** · accepted 23 November
- *"I like to read, when I can stay awake long enough."*
- *"On rest days I try to get out of the workshop for a while."*

**6. Things that have happened to her** · 23 November
- *"I came to the city when I was fifteen."*
- *"I'm the first alchemist in my family. Mum still isn't sure what I do
  all day."* (Phil: ok)
- *"I made journeyman last spring. It still doesn't feel real."* (Phil: ok)
- *"I went with the guild to the coast once, gathering salts. It was the
  first time I'd seen the sea."* (Phil: fine)

Withdrawn, and why: *"I failed my first journeyman test"*, *"I burned my
hand badly in my first year"*, *"I mislabelled a whole shelf of jars"* and
*"My master took me on after two others turned me down"* all made her
smaller, the last reading as worse than that beside her being a woman.
Hob's past is ordinary; hers had become a list of failings. Phil: *"you kind
of went with some offensive male chauvinistic ideas there."* Also
withdrawn: keeping her first potion unused (a potion is made to be used),
keeping the flask for the deposit, *"the first in my family to join a
guild"* (her mother may well be in one), and an apprentices' prize with no
time to it.

**7. Rivals and people she doesn't get on with** · accepted 23 November
- *"There's another journeyman who always seems to get the better work. I
  try not to mind."*
- *"I don't really have enemies. I'm not sure I'd know if I did."*

**Checked against what Phil corrected in Hob's notebook** (forced ties to
the call; too specific or limiting; vague yet oddly specific; a sneer;
someone believing something no one believes; regional turns of phrase;
leaving the listener to work out what she means; clever twists; and
consistency within the notebook). One fault found and fixed before
proposing: a grumble that Pip leaves her things everywhere, which
contradicted *"She's the tidy one"* in section 1. Also left out: a grumble
about the guild hall clerk, which is one of Sella's in the record, and a
grumble about the delivery man, which would have been tied to the call.

**Phil's rulings on the portions put to him, 23 November:** *"Tom too, I
suppose"* is fine · the price grumble did not say why it affects her; it now
does (she explains the bill) · gossip she shares and then withholds made no
sense; replaced · the reading joke stays (*"Jokes don't always land"*) ·
the burned hand reworded · rivals left as they are.

**Wren's notebook is complete.** Phil, 23 November: *"By not raising them
as issues, I was passing them."*

**Checked against Hob and Wren, and Phil's corrections to them:** nothing

repeats the other two customers' (a cheaper rival across town would have
copied Hob's, and was replaced by the landlord).

## Requests — any customer can be handed any of these

Each is mine **[C]** unless marked, written first as part of one customer
and moved here on 23 November. What a customer knows about a request comes
from the request and from who they are: a smith knows a sword's mark; a
shop owner has her order number to hand; a journeyman may not.

- **POOF Instant Repair** **[P, the type]** — an item sent for repair comes
  back mended, but it isn't the one that was sent.
- **Ingredients** **[P, the type]** — an ingredients order arrives one
  container short.
- **POOF Instant Order** **[P, the type]** — TLC's notice says the order
  was delivered; nothing arrived.
- **The party bag** — an item can't be transferred from a party's shared
  bag to a member's (Sella's call; causes in `docs/plan-two-causes.md`).

Some requests fit some customers more naturally than others (a guild orders
ingredients; a company sends its blades for repair), but none is tied to
one customer.
