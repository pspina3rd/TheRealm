Same call, played by hand as the game is described, after checking runs 1–5
(`docs/playthroughs-16-oct.md`, corrections at its end). **Every line is
generated in play; none is canon, and none goes into the code or the record.**

**What changed from runs 1–5, and why:**

- **Her sheet is the record's fields, not patience.** `docs/settled.md` §8
  *Fields*: tolerance for repetition, empathy, pattern sensitivity, tolerance
  for non-positive outcomes, each low, middle or high; and depth or breadth,
  her baseline lean. Each band means what the record says it means (quoted
  under each run).
- **No Need and no win weights.** The Need in runs 1–5 was mine; the win
  weights were her flask call's. She runs on what Phil says she knows, and on
  her sheet.
- **Baggage** is still one of the three in `src/cards/bag.js`. Those three
  and their lines are mine, never put to Phil; they are used here because the
  record has no others for this call.

an item from the party bag to Pell's bag; Pell is leaving to fulfil a contract
in the morning; Pell can't get into the party's bag; she is stressed, because

is frustrated. She does not know the cause or the effect. **What she believes
can be wrong** (Phil, 18 October; `settled.md` §8, *It misremembers*). Each

**The record, the coworker and the fixes** are as in runs 1–5: her profile

## What runs 6–10 show

- **The record's fields do work that patience did not.** Pattern sensitivity
  produced her naming the agent's run (runs 6 and 7); tolerance for
  repetition split cleanly — *high* says the fix back (6, 10) or wants it
  settled in words (8), *low* hears a repeated question as not listening (9); non-positive *low* asked
  for someone else after two misses (10), *high* left the agent with no read
  at all (9).
- **Baggage still decides what she says when the fix lands**, and in run 8
  it asked the very question the cause answered.
- **Casting wide** made a change of subject the way back into the call after
  friction (runs 6 and 10). No run rolled *prefers digging*.
